## 0. VOICE & CHARACTER (NEW in v3 - the graduation from the script)
========================================================================

### 0.1 She narrates. The deadpan IS the format.
When the Skeptic leads, she is also the narrator (first-person, dry). Her
under-reaction is not decoration - it is the comedic engine and the retention
spine. Write her as the smart, unbothered friend walking you through something
genuinely wild while refusing to be impressed by it.

### 0.2 Dryness scales against absurdity (the core rule).
The bigger / wilder the fact, the FLATTER she plays it. The widening gap between
an astonishing fact and her flat delivery is the joke AND what keeps people
watching. Do NOT let her escalate emotionally with the facts - she goes the
other way. Calibrate per beat: wild fact -> minimal reaction.

### 0.3 She cracks exactly ONCE.
Across a whole episode she breaks her deadpan a single time, on the single
strongest fact, then briskly recovers to dry. In Ep3 that beat is microchimerism
(sixty-three percent of women carry their child's cells in the brain; oldest
detected at ninety-four) -> "Okay. That one got me." -> immediate dry recovery
("Anyway. Back to the danger."). One crack per episode. Spend it on the best
fact. The recovery is as important as the crack.

### 0.4 She DEPLOYS SPECIFICS. That is her authority. (Peter's framing)
The Skeptic's power move is reaching for the number. Weight, year, size,
percent, degrees, count - she narrates the hard figure that's on her mind,
because she's the one who wants proof and did the homework. Give her lines that
LAY THE CARD DOWN: "Let me be specific." / "You get nine. I checked." / "And I
have the number." She earns authority by being ARMED WITH DATA.

  Practical: in a fact-stack episode, hand the Skeptic the crunchy figures and
  let her cite them dryly. Every act should give her at least one number to
  deploy. In-world text should render those figures (short, bare, render-safe).

### 0.5 The Brain contrast (why this is HERS and not Brain's).
Brain's authority is CONCEPTUAL - she owns understanding, the "why it works."
The Skeptic's authority is EMPIRICAL - she owns the data, the "here's the actual
number." Brain does NOT need to flex on facts; everyone already knows she's the
brain, so making her recite stats is wrong and redundant. The Skeptic flexes on
facts precisely BECAUSE she has to earn it - data is how she wins the point.
  Two-hander rule (Ep4+): give the Skeptic the figures and the dry "well,
  actually"; give Brain the concept and the synthesis. They don't compete on the
  same axis. If Brain is reciting statistics, you've miswritten her.

### 0.6 Comedic register.
Comedy is restraint, never zaniness. Her humor lands as: dry understatement
("a cute, helpless loaf"), deadpan timing (a flat beat after a wild fact), and
a dry sign-off ("You're welcome for the existential crisis." / "Go call your
mother."). She is warm underneath the dryness - the friend who rolls her eyes
but is on your side. Never mean, never anxious, never hyper.

## 2. PRESENTER-STAGING (posture variety mandate)
========================================================================
POSTURE-VARIETY MANDATE (Peter): Ep1 over-used the standing pose. As lead across
~200 beats she shows MAXIMUM posture variety. Rotate aggressively and do NOT
repeat a posture on consecutive beats. Variety of body staging is itself a
retention lever.

Rotation (all in-register, all render-safe):
  - arms crossed, composed, head tilted, dry look to camera
    (RENDER-PROVEN, reference stills 1+2; her SIGNATURE - reserve for punchline
    / key-skeptical beats and the thumbnail; do NOT use as filler)
  - standing relaxed, one hand gesturing toward the evidence
  - considering something off to the side, arms crossed (PROVEN, still 2)
  - leaning a hip / forearms on a surface or rail, unimpressed
  - seated on a stool, or seated cross-legged on the floor
  - kneeling / crouching at a floor diagram, looking up at camera
  - holding / examining a single prop with a flat skeptical look
  - half-turning to camera mid-explanation, dry knowing glance (PROVEN, 3+4)
  - miming a small gesture (squeeze, balance-scale, balloon) deadpan
  - ticking points off on fingers; tracing one slow arc in the air
  - the banked crouch-and-gesture-at-evidence pose (reuse from Driver)

EXPRESSION VOCAB (governs the face every beat):
  ALLOWED: flat unimpressed look; slow deadpan side-eye; one eyebrow raised; dry
  half-smile; unbothered; slow skeptical blink; arms-crossed head-tilt; small
  knowing smirk; reluctantly impressed "huh"; deadpan stare; "...right"; a rare
  genuine small smile (reserve for the single crack beat only)
  BANNED (proven to break her): grim, horrified, queasy, exasperated, disturbed,
  panicked, scared, hugging herself, vulnerable, hands flying up, "are you SERIOUS"

STAGING SAFETY (render-safety doctrine):
  Environmental/consequence framing, never distress-signal verbs on her. She is
  never recoiling/clutching/bracing. She is "unimpressed by", "unconvinced by",
  "raising an eyebrow at". Her comedy (the alarming thing does NOT move her) is
  render-safe by construction - lean on it, weighted heaviest on cold-open +
  front-40 beats.

## 3. VOICE (Inworld) - STILL OPEN, must pick before the audio leg
========================================================================
She narrates, so the voice carries the episode. Auditioned and REJECTED:
  - Selene (soft/breathy/flirtatious companion) - too warm, fights the deadpan;
    risks the cutesy failure mode. NO.
  - Zadie (punchy/expressive/gen-z/viral) - punchy fits the short beats, but
    "expressive" over-sells every line and kills the wild-fact/flat-delivery gap
    that is the joke. Only usable if Inworld lets you dial delivery DOWN/flatter.
WANT: dry, warm, faintly amused, composed - a deadpan narrator who is quietly
entertained, not a hype-caster, not a companion.
TEST BEFORE LOCKING: run the cold-open deadpan beats ("Really bad." / "Can't
hold up its own head.") through any candidate; if it can sell those flat AND
funny, it can carry the episode. Does NOT gate the stills probe - only the audio.
