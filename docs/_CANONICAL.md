The production system is the moat; the discipline of banking failures as tool-agnostic laws is the deeper moat. **A video design is PURELY DATA** — one row per beat, every decision a column; the code stays dumb and reads columns. Proven twice on 26 Jul with two fresh-session authored videos through the full gate chain.

Portfolio thesis (banked 26 Jul): the flywheel is **universal-question-answering-from-lore** — one engine, N single-lore channels, one question ledger reused across all. 20. **Canon-token naming (29 Jul evening receipt): letters and underscores only, never digits.** `audit_script.py`'s `shot_scale_category()` strips a token from a phenomenon string with the regex `^\{[a-z_]+\}\s*` — no digits in the character class. A token like `calendar_number_364` silently fails that strip, the whole phenomenon string (brace and all) falls through to the `"other"` shot-scale bucket, and every beat using that token becomes indistinguishable to the SHOT-SCALE REPETITION hard gate regardless of how varied its actual distance/framing wording is. `csv2script.py`'s own `TOKEN_RE` (`\{([a-z0-9_]+)\}`) DOES allow digits — the two tools disagree, and the stricter one (audit's) is the one that silently breaks rather than errors. Renamed `calendar_number_364` → `calendar_numeral` mid-session once traced. Check every new canon token against `[a-z_]+` before it goes in `canon.json`, not just against `csv2script.py`'s more permissive pattern.
21. **Audit's word-count is an approximation; compile's is the real gate (29 Jul evening receipt).** `audit_script.py`'s `word_count()` explicitly strips `"--"` before counting (`text.replace("--", " ")`) — its own docstring calls this "parser counting rule approximation." `csv2script.py`'s actual enforcement is `len(narr.split())`, unfiltered — a bare `"--"` token counts as one full word there. Five beats in Part II's `master.csv` sat at exactly 55 words by audit's count (PASS) and 56 by the compiler's real count (COMPILE FAIL) purely because each contained one or two ASCII-converted em-dashes. **Any beat audit reports at 53-55 words AND contains `"--"` needs a manual check against the compiler's stricter rule, or audit_script.py needs its own filter removed to stop lying about the real ceiling** — not yet decided which fix is correct; flagged for the change queue (§9).
22. **Composition-archetype law (30 Jul, Part III Studio receipt).** Every variety gate to date operates per-token; the viewer sees per-COMPOSITION. Multiple tokens sharing one composition archetype evade every gate simultaneously: Part III passed token-share, tableau-run, and near-duplicate gates and still delivered ~80 beats of "seated authority above subordinate figures" across four different throne tokens, plus 22+ of "man reads document beside fire with onlookers" — the exact two complaints inside an otherwise best-film-yet verdict. Standing rules: (a) every canon token carries an `archetype` tag in the chop config; (b) `chop_river.py` reports archetype shares and flags any archetype over the cap (default 25%) plus any untagged token; (c) **VARIANT-BANK SPANNING RULE** — every token's variant bank must span at least three composition classes: wide tableau, object/detail close (objects hold emotion better than faces), single figure or hands, environment-only. The fix for a dominant archetype is never "remove the subject" (the throne IS the book; the genre pull is real) — it is vary how the subject is SEEN.
23. **Move-distribution law (30 Jul).** The `move` column is assigned by pure distribution — `assign_moves_fuzzy()` in `chop_river.py`: equal shares of push/pull/crane/settle, max run 3, deterministic seed — never by content heuristics. Receipt: Part II shipped 83.6% push because narrow keyword/token conditions rarely matched and "push" was both the close-up default AND the unconditional fallback; Part III under the fuzzy law confirmed visibly varied on screen, 30 Jul. **With this, the motion thread is CLOSED**: three engine bugs (move-drop, push/pull freeze, duration-order) all patched and field-confirmed at kling-12 where 298/310 beats ride the floor, plus this authoring law. Worry's "unexplained motion" closes by the kling-coverage explanation (rich kling motion survives duration-stretch; linear floor zoompan does not) — no fourth bug exists.
24. **DREAM LICENSE (31 Jul, Giants Studio receipt — the Inception effect).** Declaring a dream (or vision) in the opening pre-forgives render hallucination, apocryphal weirdness, and non-realism for the audience — the frame itself grants creative license, and the weirder the spectacle inside it, the better it plays (receipt: Giants' dream-garden/scale-reveal cold open, Peter's verdict "loved it," giant's-eye close-ups singled out). Use deliberately whenever a spoke wants creative license beyond strict-photoreal plausibility: name the dream/vision in the first beats, then spend the license on spectacle. The audience wants spectacle, not dusty books on lecterns. This is a MECHANISM, not a template — vary the dream's content per the Opening Signature Ledger like any other cold open.
25. **Tag-word narration trap + gate parity (31 Jul, Jubilees box-verify receipt).** `parse_script.py` matches `MOVE:`/`VISUAL:`/`MOTION:` with `search()`, not a line anchor — narration containing any of those words followed by a colon (receipt: "in a single camera move: the angel's finger…") gets EATEN as a directive, leaving the beat wordless; the box's `create_project` wordless-verify caught what all four session-side gates missed. Until the parser is line-anchored (patch queued): **the strings `move:` `visual:` `motion:` are FORBIDDEN mid-narration** (scan: `(?i)\b(move|visual|motion)\s*:` — one hit in 911 beats across four films). Empty-narration and tag-word HARD checks are queued for `audit_script.py`/`intake.py`. **v2 is immune to this whole bug class by construction (no .md, no prose-vs-directive ambiguity).**
26. **Paid-API resource strings are case-exact (31 Jul, teaser receipt).** Inworld 404'd on voice `elliot`; `Elliot` (the channel.json spelling) worked — the endpoint hadn't moved, the resource name had a capital letter. Voice IDs, model IDs, endpoint slugs: copy them byte-for-byte from the live, working config, never normalize case.
27. **Small-N montage authoring (31 Jul, teaser receipt).** Under ~25 beats, the 15% tableau cap equals ~3 beats, so ANY token serving two paragraphs trips it: teaser/trailer authoring is MONTAGE mode — approximately one route per token, more tokens than a feature would use, spread thin. Also: a river with no `## `-act headers routes everything to block 0 (chop derives blocks from headers) — even a one-act short needs at least one header after the cold open.
28. **NEVER SIMULATE CONTACT PHYSICS (31 Jul, teaser Studio receipt — the motion failure-class law).** Image-to-video models MORPH; they cannot simulate rigid-body contact. Any motion prompt asking for an impact, breakage, emergence-through, or collision produces AI slop regardless of register (receipts: teaser beat 1 "spade drives down... dust jumping from the impact" -> exploding sand geyser; beat 2 "jar cracks open and the books slide out" -> book phasing through intact clay). The real line is not subtle-vs-spectacular or dream-vs-witness: it is **continuous media vs rigid-body interaction**, and the second class is banned everywhere, in every register, forever. Sub-principles, all standing:
   (a) **Continuous phenomena only:** fire, water, smoke, drifting dust, cloth, light, weather, breath, crowd-sway — kling renders these beautifully at ANY scale, including miracle scale. A pillar of fire descending at full fury is witness-legal; the miracle can be enormous while the physics stays coherent.
   (b) **Witness-motion grammar:** in witness register, motion prompts are camera-led + ambience-only — a named move carries the drama; in-scene verbs restricted to drifts/settles/stirs/breathes/catches/trembles; BANNED verbs: jumps/cracks/explodes/slides out/bursts/shatters/strikes. Every witness motion prompt ends with the physics anchor: "natural documentary physics, restrained realistic motion."
   (c) **Events live in CUTS, not clips:** any contact event (the strike, the opening, the shattering) is authored as a before/after still PAIR on adjacent beats — the cut carries the event, kling animates each side's atmosphere only. A century of film grammar, free.
   (d) **Dream/vision license (Law 24) is unchanged but SCOPED:** each film's Step-1 architecture carries an explicit register map naming which sequences hold transformation license; everything else runs witness grammar.
   (e) **Kling placement is on PHENOMENA, never collisions.** Front-N cold opens are architected so the front beats are atmosphere/scale; longer term the authored `kling` column (v2 door, fix-commit) replaces blind front-N with placement-by-value: Tier 1 first-ten-minutes saturation (sampler window), Tier 2 act-opener pairs (a chaptered marathon has SIXTEEN first impressions — every chapter head is a mini cold open where scrubbers re-decide), Tier 3 hero miracles/visions, plus a small probe/re-roll reserve. Uniform kling is a texture, not a language — contrast IS the craft value; emphasis is a budget, spent where memory forms.
   (f) **Probe-first at flagship counts:** the three riskiest motion prompts render before the batch commits ($1.26 to know before $10+).
29. **THE MUTE-STILL TEST (31 Jul, Jubilees beat-1/beat-2 Studio receipt — the still-directing law).** Concept-spectacle is NOT render-spectacle: every cold-open variant and every hero beat must be spectacular **with the sound off and the motion frozen** — if the wonder lives only in the caption (the narration, the metaphor, the motion), the image is a wheat field. Receipts: Jubilees beat 1 — "history as surveyed fields of years" is a magnificent IDEA whose literal substrate rendered as a farmland aerial with haze (plus model-invented observer silhouettes); beat 2 — "one field is on fire" rendered as a literal crop-fire smoke plume, an agricultural accident, not time burning. The authoring fix for grid/metaphor concepts is to make the SUBSTRATE itself otherworldly — fields of light, seams glowing like magma, a horizon that curves wrong, scale no terrestrial landscape has — so the metaphor survives while the frame stops being stock footage. Apply at Step 1 (architecture: every cold-open and hero token's variants judged as silent frames before authoring) and at the chop (read each cold-open phenomenon and ask: is THIS frame, mute and frozen, a thumbnail? If it needs the voiceover to be interesting, rewrite the substrate). No more exploding farmlands.

**Prohibitions (each one happened on 26 Jul):**

- NO touching `packaging/*`, no commits, no pushes — code transport is Peter's job.
- NO hand-authoring the compiled `.md` — it is the machine's output format.
- NO relitigating Law 8 / front-N; no guessing CLI signatures (read `--help` or the source).
- NO infrastructure questions to Peter that the briefcase answers; an unanswerable question is a defect report against this doc, filed in session notes.

**The two gate vocabularies are DIFFERENT INSTRUMENTS — never conflate:**
- *Step 1 architecture gates* (human-judged, inline): arch self-score, spectacle share, ten-frames test, subject ledger, scale budget, visual budget. These are judgment, applied before beats exist.
- *Step 3 `audit_script.py` gates* (mechanical, on the CSV): words ≤55 (band 15–25 by law), kling-words ≤18, unresolved tokens, novelty ≥2/block, topic-mix ≥70%, human-absent-run ≤5, verb top-3 ≤30%, render-safety word scan (e.g. "dying"/"blood" can silently trip the image safety classifier — reword when flagged), CSV integrity/quoting.
- *Visual-monotony gate* (intake, on the CSV — the "no 16 wheat fields" + tableau-budget enforcement): flags any BARE token (a `{token}` that is the entire visual), any run of ≥4 identical token-stripped visuals, any tableau above 15% of beats (hard; warn >10%), and any same-tableau run above 6 consecutive (hard; warn >4). Bare tokens, identical runs, and dominant tableaux are the die-video and Job-video failure signatures; caught here before spend, not after.

The write → machine-check → fix → present loop per authored chunk is the proven working pattern; run it every chunk. Craft laws that the checks serve: contents-not-containers; positive-statement-only visuals (no "no X" phenomenon language); one subject per beat; the spine object with the ten-frames test; cold open is the highest-leverage sixty seconds.

**VISUAL NOVELTY — the "no more 16 wheat fields" law (27 Jul, v2 after the Job ash-heap failure).** Tokens and canon are valuable — a recurring named figure, a location that genuinely recurs. Keep them, in moderation. But two documented failure modes now exist, and the second falsified our first fix, so read both:

FAILURE 1 (die video, 26 Jul): sixteen consecutive beats carried a bare `{sower_field}` token as the ENTIRE visual — sixteen byte-identical prompts, a minute of near-identical wheat fields. Rule: **never a bare token; every beat carries its own distinct framing** after the token (angle, distance, foreground, light).

FAILURE 2 (Job video, 27 Jul — the harder lesson): varied framing does NOT rescue a dominant tableau. `{job_ashes}` appeared on 45 of 158 beats (28% of the film) with 45 genuinely different framing suffixes — and still rendered as an endless ash-heap carousel, because **the AI re-rolls the actor and set on every still**: each return produced a different man on a different heap. Long dwells give you repetition AND inconsistency at once — a carousel of strangers, not film coverage. Real film survives 40 shots on one set because it is one actor on one set; we have neither lock. The AI-native grammar is therefore the OPPOSITE of studio film grammar: **dwell briefly, return sparingly.**

**THE TABLEAU BUDGET (hard law, enforced by intake):** a tableau = a token's location+figure. Per film: no single tableau above **15% of beats** (hard; 10% is the soft ceiling) and no same-tableau run above **6 consecutive beats** (hard; 4–6 is the dwell window — then CUT AWAY, to a memory, an illustration, an object, a landscape, and return later if needed). Calibration receipts: Job 28%/run-7 and Forgive 31%/run-21 fail loudly; the healthy videos sit at ≤12% with runs ≤4.

**SPINE RIDER:** the spine object is a THREAD, not a home base. It recurs to bind the film — a beat here, a beat there — it is not the set the film lives on. 45 returns is spine-overdose.

**SPECTACLE INVENTORY (hard authoring rule, Step 1 — before any beats are written):** the authoring session MUST make an explicit inventory of the visual spectacle the source material itself offers — scan the actual text and list its scenes, objects, creatures, weather, cosmic events — and the architecture must draw from that inventory for its cutaway and variety material. State the inventory inline for sign-off as part of Step 1. Do not gravitate to the easy anchor. Receipt: the Job video parked on the ash heap for 45 beats while the whirlwind chapters — the war-horse, the morning stars singing, leviathan, the ostrich, the storehouses of snow, the most visually spectacular catalogue in scripture — sat in the source nearly unused. The Bible's spectacle was underused in favor of the couch. The source always knows more shots than the anchor; inventory it first, then architect from it.

**IMAGERY BAN LIST (29 Jul evening, series-wide, standing — extend, don't replace, per-spoke bans as they're found):** no ironmongery/forge/workshop imagery, ever; no dusty scrolls on bookshelves; no old books on lecterns; maximum ONE wide-angle village shot per film; maximum ONE "people standing on a mountaintop swearing an oath" shot per film; NO people drawing signs in the sand with angelic shadows cast on walls; maximum ONE Mount Hermon shot per film, only if the content genuinely needs it. Receipt: these five images had become a reflexive default pulled from Part I's own human-scene inventory and applied to Part II's astronomy content regardless of fit — the real underlying rule is not "these five images are bad," it's **each spoke draws only from its own package's spectacle inventory, never borrows a prior spoke's signature images as filler.** The five are banned because they were caught doing exactly that; the next five will be different images with the same underlying failure if the real rule isn't followed.

**OPENING SIGNATURE LEDGER (29 Jul evening, series-wide, new).** The condemnation-family cold-open law (below) governs a *feeling* — daylight, oppression, the episode's specific content named or shown — not a *scene*. As originally written and applied, it was read as a literal template (bonfire, tribunal, elder, crowd) and Part II's first cold-open draft restaged Part I's actual shipped images almost beat for beat, caught only because Peter had the rendered Part I open beside the draft. Fix: bank every spoke's actual cold-open concept — one line, naming vantage/scale/staging — in the slate the moment it's authored, and check every future spoke's opening against the ledger before drafting; reject a match on vantage, blocking, or central image regardless of how well it executes the underlying law. Ledger so far: **Part I** — ground-level town square, human tribunal, human scale throughout (concealment-family retired before this, condemnation-family originated here). **Part II** — orbital/planetary vantage, cosmic scale collapsing to one human figure, tribunal reduced to a four-word smash-cut inside a much larger sequence. Whatever Part III opens with, it cannot be either of those two shapes again.

STANDING WARNING: if tableau discipline + spectacle inventory do not fix this class of failure, tokens get removed from the authoring system entirely — they will have proven a crutch that spoils content. This is the last iteration before that.

**RIVER-FIRST AUTHORING (29 Jul) — the default authoring sequence, replacing simultaneous prose+data.** Diagnosed from a direct comparison: pre-LEGO straight-to-prose authoring read as a flowing river; CSV-authored, beat-by-beat writing — even passing every existing gate — read as "bottles of water on a shelf." Cause: writing continuous prose and simultaneously tracking word-count-per-beat, canon tokens, and scale/topic budget are two different cognitive jobs; authoring in isolated chunks, tracked by token-frequency counters, catches literal repetition but is blind to *compositional* repetition (three different tokens that are secretly the same "person + craft + firelight" shot). Fix: two genuinely separate passes.
- **Pass 1 — RIVER.** Pure continuous narrative prose. No CSV thinking during drafting: no token brackets, no per-beat word counts, no SSML. Length is checked once as a whole-document target, the way a novelist checks pace against a book length — not accounted beat by beat. The draft is explicitly allowed to run long ("permission-slip" length); real editorial cutting happens in the chop, not the draft. Craft influences to invoke deliberately, matched to the job: scene-first staging of source material as cinema (Tom Holland is the standing genre reference), chapter-ending hook discipline as a learnable device (Erik Larson), muscular oral cadence for scripture-adjacent prose, read before drafting as a ritual (Robert Alter), cosmic-scale wonder reserved for genuinely cosmic material (Sagan/Attenborough register), named historical figures written as people not footnotes (Fry/Miller). Scholarly sources (Ehrman, Pagels, etc.) are research voice, never prose voice.
- **Pass 2 — CHOP.** The finished river is broken into beats at its own natural seams (sentence clusters, paragraph turns) — never forced toward a preset word target. LEGO instrumentation (canon token, VISUAL/phenomenon, scale, subject, move, air, topic_class) is layered on per resulting beat here, not during drafting. Register-specific word-band targets (15-25 wpb Line B, ~40-55 wpb golden-pair) are applied at THIS stage, shaping the chop, never constraining the draft.
- **Narration/VISUAL split happens at chop time, not draft time.** Don't think about the audio/visual channel split during Pass 1 — write full scene-setting prose as a novelist would. Per resulting beat: NARRATION keeps what a documentary voice would plausibly say (story, stakes, argument); VISUAL harvests the same river sentence's sensory material and pushes it further into technical framing language nobody would say aloud. Rule: narration must never re-describe what the image already shows (complement, never echo — the same logic as thumbnail-vs-title, one layer deeper). VISUAL is free to overlap with the source sentence's content; nobody hears it.
- **Pass 2.5 — READ (new mandatory step, before AUDIT — see §4).** A full sequential read-through of the assembled draft, start to finish, by the author, as continuous prose — the qualitative twin of the quantitative `words ÷ 165` check. This catches what token-level checks structurally cannot: repeated rhetorical crutches, flattened rhythm, pacing that drags without a single repeated word. Receipt: a concatenation-and-scan of the original Part I's narration column surfaced "Which is..." opening 8.9% of all beats and attribution crutches ("the text says/insists") recurring roughly every 6-7 beats — invisible to every existing gate. **A cheap mechanical pre-check for the worst of this (opener n-gram frequency, attribution-phrase frequency) is proposed but NOT YET BUILT into `audit_script.py`** — see §9 change queue. The human read remains necessary regardless; it catches flow/pacing problems no phrase-frequency count can.

**TWO-ZONE SCALE — cold open vs. story escalation (29 Jul, code change needed, NOT YET enforced).** The cold open is allowed to register above the story's own 1-5 escalation ceiling — the felt effect of a scale beyond the normal band, delivered entirely through prose intensity and shot framing, not a literal number typed into the scale column (the gate's DIAL 3 check hard-matches the literal string `"5"` for its peak-detection warning; typing a literal 7 or 8 makes the gate blind to it, not more aware). Act 1 onward resets to the normal 1-5 curve and escalates from there — the cold open and the story's own arc are two separate zones, not one continuous number line. Per Law 9's own principle (rules are checked mechanically or they are not checked): this needs `audit_script.py`'s DIAL 3 escalation check extended to understand a designated cold-open block as its own zone, excluded from the Act-1-onward escalation comparison, before this can be trusted as anything more than authoring intent.

**THUMBNAIL-PAYOFF LAW (29 Jul).** Beat 1's VISUAL must contain the literal imagery the thumbnail promises, not merely gesture toward it or delay it. Receipt: the original Part I's thumbnail promised fire; its actual beats 1-3 were a hand lifting a flagstone in dim lamp-glow, with fire not appearing until beat 4 — the single most-flagged defect in that video's post-render critique. The fix costs nothing: architect the cold open so its very first beat contains the thumbnail's central image.

**PAUSE DOCTRINE (breath — required authoring judgment, per video).** Elliot's pace is right, but the narration must be given space to breathe or it runs airless. Sprinkle Inworld SSML break tags throughout the script — `<break time="700ms" />` inside the narration column, at the natural resting points. This is a per-video judgment call on *placement*, but it is NOT optional to do: every script gets deliberate pausing. Range and placement heuristics (observed working, 26 Jul — ~60 breaks across ~158 beats is a healthy density): a beat of ~0.9–1.5s (900–1500ms) AFTER the title question or a hero line lands, to let it register; ~0.6–0.7s (600–700ms) at the turn between thoughts or before a reveal; ~0.4–0.5s (450–500ms) on quick connective beats. Longer holds pair naturally with `settle`/`static` moves and hush/awe registers; short breaths with `push`/`pull` momentum beats. Do not front-load all pauses into the cold open — distribute across the whole runtime so the teaching body breathes too. Breaks live in the narration text and are stripped before the word-count, so they cost nothing against the word band.

## 6. MOTION DOCTRINE (folded whole — drives the `move` column and kling MOTION prompts)
**Motion is a function of the shot, not a choice.** Per beat, in order: force is vertical/rising → **crane**. One overwhelming subject → **push**. Meaning is scale/number/spread → **pull**. Quiet beat (grief, aftermath, held breath) → **settle** (slow exhale down) or **static/near-locked** (ambient only) — the quiet override BEATS the axis rule. Else → **push** (safe default).
Hard rules: one primary motion per beat; slow is fast; lock the subject, move the camera; **never push on silence**; motivated, never timer-driven; the still is locked first (composition can't drift — only the move is variable). Healthy full-film distribution: push ~25–30%, pull ~20%, crane ~20%, static ~15–20%, settle ~10–15%; max same-move run ≤2–3. Rotation comes free from the shots' own variety.
