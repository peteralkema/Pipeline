## 0. The binding constraint, named

**1,000 subscribers.** Watch hours (503/4,000 at day 44, compounding off a long-form catalogue)
will clear first on run rate. Subs at 1.9/day is ~16 months organically. A single breakout
collapses both bars (Discernment: 0→10K subs in 45 days on five hits). Therefore:

> **Every decision is scored on breakout probability per Peter-hour.**
> That is draws × per-draw quality. Per-draw quality lives in Step 0
> (topic · title · thumbnail · first 60s) — the cheapest steps in the system.

Corollaries, all from measured data:
- Execution is at ceiling (Methuselah: 86/100). Marginal execution effort returns ~nothing.
- Mid-film retention is 0.70–0.75 relative — above median. The middle is not the problem.
- The measured weaknesses are the first minute (0.22–0.36 relative) and the click (§7b:
  CTR-starved, not distribution-starved). Both are Step 0/Step 9 items.
- Outcomes are heavy-tailed. Hit rate in this lane is maybe 5–15%. No single video is
  evidence of anything; judge cohorts of 40+, never uploads of one.

## 1. The false dilemma, resolved

LEGO vs PIPELINE is not a choice. They are **two product lines with different jobs**:

| | **Line B — batch** | **Line F — feature** |
|---|---|---|
| system | SCRIPT-CONTRACT → PIPELINE (unattended) | LEGO 0–10 (+ Filmora by hand) |
| product | 10–25 min question-videos | 30–40+ min films |
| job | **the draw machine** — volume of tickets | **the watch-hour engine + flagship craft** |
| unit cost | ~$3–8, near-zero Peter-time post-authoring | ~$100–160 + 2–4 sessions of Peter-time |
| cadence | daily, without exception | **max 1–2/month** |
| evidence | Forbidden Books (batch-made) is the channel's best watch-hour asset AND best sub-rate | WITW/Methuselah unread; the line is unproven on distribution |

**Scope law (fixes the doc ambiguity):** SCRIPT-CONTRACT governs Line B authorship.
LEGO governs Line F. One channel, two lines, no overlap.

The pick (100→40) stays human — it is the creative act and that is settled. What is capped
is everything else feature-shaped: probes beyond the proven config, convergence passes past
the noise floor, and any craft work that moves a film from 86 toward 90 while its
architecture or package sits below the bar.

## 2. The topic gate (graduated from 24 Jul — the biggest lever found)

Every script, both lines, is tagged **`universal` or `lore`** at authoring, decided by one test:

> **Could someone who has never heard of the Book of Enoch want to watch this?**

- Universal = a question the viewer already carries (where does evil come from · what happens
  when you die · why do we speak different languages · what does God look like). The source is
  the EVIDENCE. Enoch is the vehicle, never the subject.
- Lore = a question you can only have once inside the material (which angel taught what ·
  the 200 names · female Nephilim).
- Evidence: within-operator, on channels WITH distribution, universal beats lore by
  100–800× under identical packaging (Discernment 131K vs 154; Enoch Codex 293K vs 12K —
  and the 293K is an ENOCH video: the frame was lore, the question was universal).
- **Batch composition: ≥70% universal.** Lore stays as catalogue depth, never the lead.
- This gate runs IN ADDITION to LEGO Step 0's winnability gate, not instead of it.
- **The experiment is judged at N≥40 tagged videos** — group means, universal vs lore,
  on our own catalogue. Until then it is a well-evidenced hypothesis, not law.

## 3. The weekly operating shape (where the hours go)

- **Daily (machine):** Line B cadence never breaks. Uploads were wrongly paused once
  (24 Jul) on a falsified thesis — under lottery dynamics fewer draws is strictly worse.
- **Peter's craft hours, in priority order:**
  1. **Step 0 across MANY videos** — authoring a batch of twenty packages (topic + title +
     thumbnail text) outranks any single video's Steps 2–8. Titles carry the question;
     thumbnails carry a DIFFERENT proposition (never the same nouns — the caption trap).
  2. **First-60s repair** — the cold-open spec (one moment, no trailer, seam rules) applied
     to Line B openings too; it is the channel's only measured retention weakness.
  3. **The feature in flight**, if any — gated hard (§4).
- **What Peter stops doing:** polishing execution past the scorecard's 86; re-litigating
  settled doctrine (WPM, register); reading single videos at 48h; competitor deep-dives
  outside the fortnightly instrument.

## 4. Line F gates (a feature earns its cost or dies at $0)

No feature enters authoring until ALL of:
- Step 0 passes the **universal test** (§2) AND the winnability gate.
- **Architecture ≥ 70** on the scorecard rubric, scored at Step 1 ON THE LEDGER,
  before a beat exists (Methuselah's entire lesson, §F of the scorecard).
- The ten-frames test on the spine object; spectacle share ≥30%; no scale-5 before
  the final third.

And a standing stop-rule: if the scorecard says architecture <70 at Step 1, the fix is a
new Step 1, never better Steps 2–8. Craft cannot rescue design — proven at $156.

## 5. Measurement (closing the loop that has never closed)

The FILM RECORD has zero distribution rows filled. The flywheel has only ever spun on the
production axis. Fix:

- **Per-video ledger row at ship** (both lines): `topic_class` · `line` · length ·
  title shape · date. One CSV, appended forever.
- **Weekly, manual, 10 minutes:** Studio read of per-video impressions + CTR (API-blind,
  accepted). Log the numbers into the ledger.
- **Fortnightly, channel level:** the five audit indicators (packaging gap vs the FIXED
  cohort — winners AND losers, Covenant Lens is the age-matched control; retention–views
  correlation; format variance; question-title rate; tag coverage). Cohorts are frozen at
  selection — re-picking each cycle is survivorship bias.
- **Per feature:** scorecard (arch/exec split) + fill the FILM RECORD distribution rows
  at 48h/14d/21d. A film with no written observation taught the next one nothing.
- **Method law (banked 24 Jul):** any claimed differentiator must be tested against
  channels that FAILED with it before it counts. Winners-only analysis produced a false
  100× thesis that survived four sessions.

## 7. What this strategy does NOT claim

It does not claim the breakout is schedulable. The honest model is: a low, uncertain hit
rate that topic class and packaging measurably raise, drawn many times at near-zero
marginal cost, with the milestone bars (not outlier channels) as the scoreboard. Forty
draws with no hit at a 5–10% rate is unremarkable, not a verdict. The edge over the
identically-placed cohort (Covenant Lens et al.) is exactly three things: **repeatability,
automation, and a measurement loop they don't run.** This file exists to make sure the
loop finally closes.
