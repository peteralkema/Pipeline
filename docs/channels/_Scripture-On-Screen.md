## 0. WHAT THIS CHANNEL IS NOW, IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Cinematic Bible **TEACHING** — not Bible movies. The felt-question channel of the portfolio: title = a universal human question in its rawest search language ("What does the Bible actually say about worry?"), body = **structured, counted teaching from the canonical text** ("[N] things [Book/Jesus] says about [concern]"), rendered ~90% inside the biblical world at bright-blockbuster photoreal spectacle. The 2 a.m. contract: someone in real distress types the question and receives real, usable teaching from the authority they already trust. Canonical scripture ONLY — apocrypha is Sacred Dawn's ground, never this channel's. Elliot voice.

**Why the pivot is winnable (validated, two-peer-group NexLev cross-check):** the movies lane is owned; the teaching lane is fragmented, and universal-question topics beat lore/insider topics 100–800× within-operator across N=6 operators. Format (long, fully-produced from video #1) is necessary but NOT sufficient — topic is the differentiator. Felt questions refill (per-person recurrence + generational refill); this is the deep-ledger lane of the entire portfolio and slot #1 of the universal-question thesis.

## 1. THE TOPIC GATE (hard, checked at Step 0)
- Universal question in the TITLE — someone who never opens a Bible still wants the answer. Widest net for cold-browse.
- Structured book-teaching in the BODY — named, counted, distinct lessons. A title promising "10 things" whose body can't fill 10 distinct, well-sequenced teachings ships shorter or dies at Step 1.
- Slate mix ≥70% universal-led.
- **Teaching-substance gate:** before spend, verify the counted lessons are real and distinct (not one idea sliced N ways). This is the moat against slop teaching channels — the career-teacher edge made mechanical.
- Ledger check: title's subject pool vs the shipped ledger (§8) — first entry takes the pot; re-entry into an adjacent pool needs a genuinely different question/book angle, consciously approved.

## 2. THE REGISTER (canon-locked, v3)
- **~90% biblical world.** The biblical world IS the show and the swarm-resistant moat; modern kitchen/stock is commodity. Modern imagery ONLY in the cold-open hook; cross into the biblical world by beat 5–6 and STAY. The modern tether thereafter is VERBAL over a biblical image (Option B).
- **Bright-blockbuster photoreal**, canon-locked: every scene forced photorealistic (kills painterly drift); cultural precision — Hebrew, not Egyptian/Roman anachronism.
- **Jesus canon law:** Jesus named/depicted ONLY in biblically-present scenes; "NOT Jesus" guard on every other robed-teacher figure (13 guards in worry canon v3 — copy the pattern).
- Voice/writing register: reverent, warm, a trusted teacher — never hype, never "BANNED/FORBIDDEN". Attribution moat: the text SAYS; we never assert beyond it.
- Reference-lock recurring figures (Elijah, David, Joseph, Daniel, any teacher-frame) for one visual identity across the slate.

## 3. FORMAT SHAPE (per _CANONICAL laws)
~17–20 min teaching = 150–200 beats at 15–25 w/beat, ≤6 thematic acts (never one-act-per-lesson; ROMAN ceiling), $25 BOM ceiling, front-N Kling 19–26 concentrated on the cold open, `move` on every beat. Lessons cluster into acts by theme; each lesson gets its own image and its own practical turn.

## 4. TITLE GRAMMAR
"[Universal question]? [Count] [Answers/Things/Lessons] [Book/Jesus] [Actually] [Says/Taught]" — ~60 chars, under 80. "Actually" is the channel's credibility signature. Question first, always.

## 5. PACKAGING
Thumbnail: one biblical scene at full spectacle, question as the lockup (caps), subtitle names the authority ("WHAT JESUS ACTUALLY SAID"). No text in the rendered image itself. Canon law applies to thumbs (Jesus only biblically-present).
