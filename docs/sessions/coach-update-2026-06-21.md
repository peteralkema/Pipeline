## 2. How the niches were researched (true to Leo's criteria)

Every channel was selected against **Leo's niche-research criteria**, applied via NexLev before any production:

1. **Under 100K subscribers** — beginner-open, not a saturated giant's lane
2. **Started in the last 1–4 months** — proof the lane is breakable *right now*, not a legacy moat
3. **Has a viral video (200K+ views)** — demonstrated demand, not a guess
4. **Growing, not declining** — momentum currently up and to the right
5. **Recreatable** — reproducible with our faceless AI cinematic pipeline

The method each time: NexLev `search_niche_finder_channels` (faceless filter, outlier-score sort) → find recently-started channels with a viral breakout under 100K subs → confirm the lane is growing → confirm we can recreate it. Then pick reference channels, study their packaging (titles/thumbnails), and build.

*(One deliberate exception — [Lazarus Films](https://www.youtube.com/@lazarus-films) — does not chase this fast-breakout profile; it's a long-horizon craft play. Flagged honestly in §3.)*

## 5. Sacred Dawn — the one channel with a real read (live NexLev data, 21 June)

**[Sacred Dawn](https://www.youtube.com/@sacredawn):** 11 days old (created 10 June), **27 subscribers, 1,823 views, 8 videos.** Small numbers — but the *shape* of the early data already validates the strategy cleanly.

**Per-video (last 28 days, retention computed from watch-time, not the unreliable launch-window field):**

| Video | Views | Avg view duration | Subs gained | Read |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **War in Heaven / Satan** (marquee Bible headline) | 539 (712 lifetime) | **140s** | **11** (most) | Best clicks + most subs, **weakest hold** |
| **The Watchers' Daughters** (apocryphal / Enoch) | 307 | **450s** (best) | 3 | **Best holder** — 3× the watch-minutes of the next video |
| Before the Flood (launch film) | 279 | 262s | 3 | Solid |
| Enoch | 134 | 407s | 0 | Strong hold |
| The Book of Giants | 146 | 392s | 2 | Strong hold |
| Cain and Abel | 42 | 309s | 0 | Newest |

**What this says (and it maps exactly to the strategy):**
- The **strange/apocryphal** topics (Watchers, Enoch, Giants) **hold viewers** — 390–450s average view duration.
- The **marquee Bible headline** (War in Heaven/Satan) **wins the click and the subscribers but leaks viewers** (140s) — a wide-net catch, not a deep hold.
- So the channel's two jobs are working as designed: broad headlines catch the click and convert subs; the strange/apocryphal content holds the committed viewer. The plan is to wire them together (marquee videos route into the deep-hold ones).

**Early qualitative signal (the rebuilt Satan flagship, published 21 June):** within a day of publish, a returning viewer ([@Solarpunk97](https://www.youtube.com/@Solarpunk97)) commented *"I've watched every single one of these! More please!"* One comment isn't data — but a viewer who has watched *every* video asking for more is exactly the committed-audience signal the strategy is built to create, showing up before the retention numbers can.

**The real test is still ahead:** the **rebuilt Satan flagship** (a full re-make of the worst-retaining video, published 21 June) is designed to *fix* that 140s hold. Its retention read is diarised for **~mid-July** — that's the proof point, not today's numbers.

**Note on [Sacred Soak](https://www.youtube.com/@SacredSoak):** launched today and not yet connected to NexLev analytics, so there is genuinely no data to report — expected for a day-old channel. It shares Sacred Dawn's proven biblical lane, tuned for lean-back/sleep listening.
