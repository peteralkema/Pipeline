## POOL A — DEATH & THE AFTERLIFE (the proven core: Discernment's 131K/91K/40K all live here)

1. **What Happens in the First Minutes After You Die? The Oldest Written Answer**
   The one question every viewer carries, answered from the oldest text that claims an eyewitness — Enoch says he was shown exactly where the dead go and what happens on arrival. The film walks his tour: the hollow mountain in the west, the four chambers, the spring of bright water, and why the dead are not all together.

2. **Is Anyone in Heaven Right Now — or Are the Dead Asleep?**
   Half of Christianity believes the dead are conscious now; the other half believes they sleep until the resurrection — most viewers have never heard there's a dispute. The film stages both readings from the actual verses, then shows what Enoch describes: souls waiting, aware, in appointed places.

3. **Do the Dead Know What Is Happening on Earth?**
   Whether a lost parent can see you now is among the most-searched spiritual questions in existence. Scripture gives three scenes that bear on it — the cloud of witnesses, the rich man who remembers his brothers, the souls under the altar asking "how long" — and the film renders each and lets them argue.

4. **Why Do Good People Die Young? The Answer Enoch Says He Was Given**
   The oldest complaint against God, put directly to an angel in the Book of Enoch — and answered. The film opens on the injustice everyone has felt at a graveside, then delivers the text's startling claim: the early dead are being taken FROM something, not robbed of it.

5. **Do Babies and Small Children Go Straight to Heaven?**
   Asked by every grieving parent in history, and the Bible never answers it in one verse — but David's four words about his dead son come closer than most people know. The film gathers every passage that touches it into one honest, consoling case.

6. **Has Anyone Ever Visited Heaven and Come Back? The Men Who Claimed They Did**
   Before modern near-death stories, scripture records four men who said they saw heaven and returned — Enoch, Isaiah, Ezekiel, Paul. The film renders each vision at full spectacle and shows the strange detail: they describe the same throne.

7. **What Sin Can Never Be Forgiven? The One Warning Jesus Ever Gave Like This**
   "Every sin will be forgiven — except one" is the most frightening sentence in the Gospels, and most believers secretly fear they've committed it. The film shows exactly what was happening when Jesus said it, and why the very fear is the evidence of innocence.

8. **Can You Talk to the Dead? The Night a King Tried It**
   Séances are a modern industry, but scripture stages one — King Saul, a forbidden medium, and the dead prophet Samuel actually appearing, to the medium's own terror. The film renders the one necromancy scene in the Bible and lets its outcome answer the question.

## POOL B — HEAVEN'S MECHANICS (the Enoch Codex 293K vein: heaven as a real, mapped place)

9. **What Is Heaven Actually Made Of? The Man Who Says He Walked Through It**
   Not clouds and harps — Enoch describes walls of crystal, floors of fire, and a house of ice that burns, in the oldest architectural tour of heaven ever written. The film rebuilds it room by room as he describes it.

10. **Will You See Your Animals Again? What Scripture Says About Beasts in Heaven**
    Whether pets have souls is dismissed by theologians and asked by everyone else. The film gathers the texts — the breath of life given to beasts, Isaiah's wolf and lamb on the holy mountain, every creature singing in Revelation — into the strongest honest case the sources allow.

11. **What Will You Actually DO in Heaven Forever? The Boredom Question Answered**
    Eternal rest sounds suspiciously like eternal boredom, and it's the quiet reason many people fear heaven more than they admit. The film shows what the texts actually describe — reigning, building, judging angels, a city with work in it — and why "rest" never meant sitting still.

12. **How Big Is Heaven? The Text That Gives Its Measurements**
    Revelation records an angel physically measuring the city — and the number is so large the city would reach past the atmosphere. The film does the arithmetic on screen at full scale, rendering a cube 1,400 miles high against the curve of the earth.

13. **Will You Still Be Married in Heaven? The Question They Used to Trap Jesus**
    Asked as a trick — one woman, seven brothers, whose wife is she? — and Jesus's answer changed how every marriage should be understood. The film stages the trap, the answer, and what "like the angels" actually means for the people you love.

14. **Does Everyone Get the Same Heaven? What the Texts Say About Rewards**
    The idea that heaven has ranks sounds like heresy and is stated plainly in the sources — treasure, crowns, one star differing from another in glory. The film explores the uncomfortable, motivating claim that eternity is not identical for everyone.

## POOL C — ANGELS & THE UNSEEN (universal door, Enoch evidence)

15. **Do You Have a Guardian Angel? What the Oldest Sources Actually Say**
    It's the most widely believed idea in folk Christianity — and the texts both support and complicate it. The film traces the evidence from Jacob's ladder to "their angels always see the face of my Father," and Enoch's claim that specific angels are assigned to watch.

16. **What Do Angels Actually Look Like? Not What's on Your Christmas Cards**
    Every angel in scripture opens with "do not be afraid" — because what appeared was wheels of fire, four faces, and beings covered in eyes. The film renders the biblical descriptions literally, at full spectacle, and shows where the wings-and-halo image really came from.

17. **Can Angels Die? The Texts That Describe Their Fate**
    They're called immortal — yet the sources describe angels bound, imprisoned, and sentenced. The film follows the strange legal drama of beings who cannot die awaiting a judgment written before the flood.

18. **Are Angels Watching You Right Now? The Book That Says Four Never Stop**
    Enoch names four beings who stand before God and never sleep, recording everything. The film renders the watchers at their posts and asks what it changes to live observed.

19. **Why Did God Create Angels First — and What Went Wrong?**
    Before there were humans there was already a society, a hierarchy, and a rebellion. The film tells the pre-human history of creation the sources describe — and why humanity may have been the answer to a war already lost.

## POOL D — GOD (the biggest questions there are)

20. **What Was God Doing Before He Created Anything?**
    A child's question that has no safe answer — and the texts are stranger than the silence suggests: wisdom present "before the beginning," a plan written before light. The film renders the before, as far as the sources dare describe it.

21. **Why Does God Stay Hidden? The Text That Explains the Silence**
    If God exists, why not simply appear? — the honest question behind most unbelief. The film gathers the answers the sources themselves give: the face no one can see and live, the still small voice after the fire, and Enoch's claim that even angels cannot look directly.

22. **Does God Hear Every Prayer? Where Your Words Actually Go**
    Enoch describes something almost no one has heard of: angels whose entire task is carrying prayers upward, and a chamber where they are kept. The film follows one prayer's journey as the text maps it — from a whisper in the dark to the throne.

23. **Does God Ever Change His Mind? The Verses That Say He Did**
    "God repented that he had made man" is in Genesis, and it detonates the tidy picture of an unchanging deity. The film faces the hard verses honestly — the flood, Nineveh, Moses arguing God down — and what the sources mean by a God who can be moved.

24. **Why Won't God Say His Name? The Word It Was Forbidden to Speak**
    There is a name so guarded that scribes washed before writing it and readers still refuse to pronounce it. The film tells the history of the four letters, the burning bush where they were given, and what "I AM" was actually claiming.

25. **Why Doesn't God Do Miracles Anymore? What Actually Changed**
    Seas split and fire fell — then, apparently, it stopped, and every believer has quietly wondered why. The film maps where the miracles cluster in scripture (three short bursts, not a constant stream), and what the texts say the silence between them is for.

## POOL E — EVIL, SUFFERING, THE ENEMY (universal door on the channel's home ground)

26. **Why Doesn't God Just Destroy Satan Now? The Delay the Texts Explain**
    If the war is won, why is the enemy still free? — the question every sufferer asks. The film renders the sources' strange answer: a sentence already passed, an appointed day already named in Enoch, and a delay that is mercy pointed at us.

27. **Can Satan Read Your Mind? What the Sources Actually Grant Him**
    Most people's fear of the devil assumes powers scripture never gives him. The film audits the enemy's real capabilities from the texts — what he can see, suggest, and accuse, and the one thing he demonstrably cannot do.

28. **Why Does God Allow Suffering? The Answer an Angel Gave Enoch**
    The oldest wound in faith, put as a direct question in the Book of Enoch — and answered, not with philosophy but with a tour: Enoch is shown where evil came from, where it is chained, and the day already fixed for its end. The film takes the same tour.

29. **Where Is Hell, Actually? The Place Enoch Says He Was Taken To See**
    Hell has an address in the oldest sources — a valley outside Jerusalem you can walk today, and a burning prison Enoch says he saw in the far west. The film maps both, the real geography and the visionary one, and how the two became one word.

30. **Why Does Evil Feel Stronger at Night? What the Ancient Texts Knew About Darkness**
    A fear older than any doctrine — and the sources treat darkness as territory, not absence: spirits that roam until dawn, a watch divided into hours, morning as rescue. The film renders the ancient night as the texts describe it.

## POOL F — ORIGINS & EDEN (everyone's questions about the start)

31. **Where Is the Garden of Eden Now? The Man Who Claims He Saw It Still Standing**
    Genesis never says Eden was destroyed — it says it was guarded, and Enoch claims he was shown it, intact, with the tree still growing. The film follows the trail: the four rivers you can still name on a map, the sealed entrance, and the promise that the tree returns at the end.

32. **What Was the Forbidden Fruit, Actually? It Was Never Called an Apple**
    The most famous fruit in history is never named in the text — the apple is a Latin pun that stuck. The film unpacks what the sources do say, what the ancient traditions guessed (fig, grape, wheat, light), and why the anonymity is the point.

33. **Why Did God Put the Tree There at All? The Question Everyone Asks First**
    If the fruit was fatal, why plant it in reach? — the first objection every child raises and most sermons dodge. The film takes it seriously through the sources: what choice means, what the second tree was for, and the reading where the tree was never a trap.

34. **Did Adam and Eve Make It to Heaven? The Fate of the First Sinners**
    The two people who broke the world — scripture never directly says where they went, but the oldest traditions do, in detail. The film follows the apocryphal afterlife of Adam: his burial, the promise made over him, and the ancient claim that the cross stood on his grave.

35. **Why Do We Wear Clothes? The Strange Answer in Genesis**
    No other animal is ashamed — and Genesis treats that shame as evidence of a catastrophe. The film reads the strangest small detail in scripture (God as the first tailor) and what the garments of skin were covering.

## POOL G — THE WORLD'S MEMORY (shared human experience, ancient evidence)

36. **Why Does Every Culture Remember a Great Flood?**
    Babylon, India, China, the Maya — hundreds of unconnected cultures preserve the same story: the water, the boat, the birds. The film lays the world's flood memories side by side with Genesis and asks the only two possible explanations.

37. **Why Do We Dream? What the Oldest Texts Say Is Actually Happening**
    A third of your life spent in another world, and science still can't say why. The film explores the ancient answer — dreams as a channel, from Jacob's ladder to the warnings that saved the Magi — and Enoch's claim that his entire book came through one.

38. **How Long Could Humans Live? The 120-Year Limit and Who Set It**
    Genesis records lifespans of nine centuries, then a spoken cap: "his days shall be a hundred and twenty years" — a ceiling modern longevity science keeps hitting. The film charts the great decline, name by name, and the verse that called the number first.

39. **Could the Flood Happen Again? The Promise — and the Warning After It**
    The rainbow is scripture's answer, and almost no one reads the next clause: never again by water. The film delivers both halves honestly — the covenant everyone knows, and the fire the texts say is held for the end instead.

40. **Who Actually Wrote the Bible? The Honest Answer**
    Forty authors, three languages, fifteen centuries — and entire books whose writers are unknown. The film tells the human story of the Bible's assembly without flinching: the scribes, the councils, the arguments over what belonged, and why the messiness is the strongest argument that no one invented it.
