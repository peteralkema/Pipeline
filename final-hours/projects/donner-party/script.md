title: They Took a Shortcut to California in 1846. The Mountains Trapped Them in the Snow for Four Months.
description: In the spring of 1846, a group of ordinary families set out for California with everything they owned. Persuaded to try an unproven shortcut, they lost crucial weeks in the desert and the mountains, and reached the Sierra Nevada just as the first heavy snows closed the pass. Trapped in makeshift camps for four months, nearly half of them died — and the survivors were driven to the unspeakable to stay alive. This is the documented story of the Donner Party: the decision that doomed them, the long winter in the snow, and the rescues that came too late for so many. Recreated with dignity from the survivors' accounts and the relief records. Would you have taken the shortcut? Tell us in the comments.
