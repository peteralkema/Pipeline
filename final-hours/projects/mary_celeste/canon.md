## Canon 2 — Mary Celeste deck (empty)

**Used in beats:** 1 (cold open), 4 (discoveries become belongings)

**Visual identity:** The deck of a small brigantine. Empty of people. Wooden planks, ropes, brass fittings. Two masts visible. Sails partly set, partly torn. The wheel turning slowly to nothing. Open North Atlantic in the background. The mood is *abandonment*, not *destruction* — everything still functional, just unattended.

**Detail vocabulary (20+ items):**

- Ropes uncoiled but not tangled, on the deck
- A torn sail seen from below
- A torn sail seen from above
- The compass binnacle (disturbed)

- The empty davits where the yawl should hang
- Neat tackle hanging in empty space
- The boarding party climbing up the side (silhouettes from below)
- Boots on the deck planks (boarding party, face-not-resolved)
- The wake of the ship from the stern
- The bow cutting through dark water
- Shadow of mast on deck planks
- Coil of rope half-undone

- The deck at dawn (cool blue)
- The deck at dusk (warmer)
- Damaged rigging silhouette against sky
- Spray hitting the bow
- A hatch cover left ajar
- The captain's wheel close on the spokes

**Lighting:** Overcast North Atlantic. December light — cold, grey, low-saturation. Occasional break of sun through cloud. Dawn for the discovery, dusk for the loaded "10 days alone" beats.

**Color palette:** Slate grey, steel blue, weathered wood brown, white sail, dark sea. Cold register throughout.

**Never show:** Any human on the deck except the boarding party (always silhouetted, face-not-resolved). The Briggs family. Any indication of life on the ship.

## Canon 8 — Open Atlantic

**Used in beats:** 1 (the empty ship sailing alone), 4 (the 10 days of drift), 5 (closer)

**Visual identity:** Just the ocean. The Mary Celeste appears as a small distant silhouette or not at all. The horizon. The waves. The sky. This canon carries the silent beats of the script — the time when the ship sails herself, when no human is visible at all.

**Detail vocabulary (12+ items):**
- The Mary Celeste in extreme long shot, a small silhouette on the horizon
- The horizon at dawn — empty
- The horizon at dusk — empty
- The horizon at night — moonlight on water
- Waves rolling toward camera
- A close-up of dark Atlantic water
- The sky reflected in the sea
- Storm clouds in the distance
- A break of sun through grey cloud
- The wake of an unmanned ship trailing into nothing
- The compass on the unmanned deck, drifting (overlaps with Canon 2)
- The empty wheel slowly turning (overlaps with Canon 2)
- Sea birds in long shot
- A bottle floating (or perhaps the absence of any wreckage — the unanswered question)

**Lighting:** Full range — dawn, day, dusk, night. The longest tonal range of any canon. Some scenes near-black with moon reflection. Some scenes pale grey overcast.

**Color palette:** Steel grey, deep navy, charcoal, the warm gold of dawn at the closer. Maximum desaturation.

**Never show:** Any human at all. Any modern element. Any rescue or arrival. This canon is the silence between events.

## Risk register

**The grandmother problem:** Canon 1 / 7 both include Arthur's grandmother. We have no documented face for her. Solution: always show her hands, her back, her silhouette. Treat exactly like Arthur — face-never-resolved. Same discipline.

**The family photograph problem:** Several canons reference a "family photograph" or "daguerreotype." We must show these as objects (the frame, the back, partial views obscured by reflection or angle) but never resolve the faces. Document this discipline explicitly in storyboard prompts.

**The Dei Gratia boarding party:** Multiple sailors with documented names (Morehouse, the boarding party). Apply same discipline — silhouettes, hands, boots. Never face-resolved.

**Lighting drift risk:** With 8 canons across a 13-min video, lighting consistency within each canon is harder to police than in Pudding Lane's 5-6 canons. Storyboard generator must lock lighting parameters per canon and not let them drift across the shot list.

## What this canon plan represents

This is the dignified register operating at the production level. Every canon enforces face-never-resolved. Every canon supports 20+ shot variations within its locked aesthetic. Every canon protects cost (no canon explosion) while supporting visual variety (deep detail vocabulary).
