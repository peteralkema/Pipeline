title: A Ship Sat Stranded on a Reef for Two Days While Rescuers Waited Nearby. Then the Storm Came Back.
description: In October 1918, the coastal liner Princess Sophia ran onto a reef in the dark waters of Alaska's Lynn Canal with more than three hundred and fifty people aboard. For nearly two days she sat there, upright and intact, while a small fleet of rescue boats gathered around her — unable to take anyone off in the heavy seas. Everyone waited for the weather to calm. Instead, the storm came back. This is the documented story of the Princess Sophia, the worst maritime disaster in the history of the Pacific Northwest, and the agonising two days when so many lives hung on a falling tide. Recreated from the wireless messages and the inquiry record. What would you have done in those two days? Tell us in the comments.
