description: In 1587, more than a hundred English colonists settled on Roanoke Island, off the coast of what is now North Carolina. Their governor, John White, sailed back to England for supplies — and a war with Spain kept him away for three years. When he finally returned in August 1590, the colony was gone. The houses were dismantled, the people vanished, and a single word was carved into a post: CROATOAN. This is the documented story of England's lost colony — what John White found, what the clues actually say, and the leading theory for where the colonists went.
