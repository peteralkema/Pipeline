## PART ONE — The Long Walk Back

[A] The return journey stretched eight hundred miles across the polar plateau and down a great glacier and across an ice shelf — all of it on foot, all of it hauling heavy sledges, all of it in cold that few human beings have ever survived. ## PART TWO — The Man Who Walked Out

[A] As they crossed the great ice shelf for home, the cold began to maim them. Frostbite took hold. Their feet rotted in their boots. [A] The three men died in that tent, in their sleeping bags, as the storm blew itself out around them. ## CLOSE
