## PART TWO - The Failure

[A] Here the disaster took its darkest turn. As the ship's peril became undeniable, order broke down. There was no firm discipline, no enforced rule of the weak first. Instead, there was panic - and in the panic, the strong took the boats.

[A] Crewmen, whose duty was to manage the boats and to serve the passengers, seized places in them for themselves. Boats pulled away from the sinking ship carrying mostly able-bodied men.
