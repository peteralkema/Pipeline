description: In January 1906, the passenger steamer Valencia ran onto the rocks off the wild west coast of Vancouver Island. She did not sink at once. For nearly two days, the survivors clung to her rigging and her tilting deck, in sight of cliffs and of would-be rescuers — who could not reach them across the deadly surf. This is the documented story of the Valencia, one of the most harrowing wrecks in the history of a coastline sailors called the Graveyard of the Pacific: the lifeboats that failed, the rescuers held back by the sea, and the long, freezing wait that almost no one survived.
