description: In April 1865, just weeks after the American Civil War ended, the Mississippi steamboat Sultana was carrying home thousands of just-released Union prisoners of war — men who had survived the battlefields and the horrors of the prison camps. Dangerously overloaded, her worn-out boilers exploded in the dark north of Memphis. It remains the worst maritime disaster in American history, and almost no one remembers it. This is the documented story of the Sultana: the men who endured everything only to die going home, the overloaded boat, and why a tragedy this vast was forgotten.
