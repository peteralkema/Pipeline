## Detail vocabulary per canon (Principle 12 production-patterns)

For storyboard editing, draw shots from these distinct angles/details within each scene canon. This prevents repetition and produces visually varied shots within locked aesthetics.

### klm_cockpit (target: 10-12 shots)
- Wide of cockpit from behind both seats (Van Zanten visible in left)
- Three-quarter angle behind left seat (single Van Zanten, right seat out of frame)
- Profile of Van Zanten in left seat looking forward
- Close on the throttle quadrant (four levers, no hand)
- Close on the brake levers in released position
- Close on the radio panel and hanging headset
- POV through the windscreen looking into fog
- Close on the airspeed indicator and engine gauges climbing
- Close on the control yoke being pulled back
- Close on the instrument panel glow on a cuff with four stripes

### pan_am_cockpit (target: 3-4 shots)
- Three-quarter behind left seat (single American captain visible)
- Close on the radio panel with the transmit indicator lit
- Single light-blue uniform sleeve reaching toward radio panel
- POV through Pan Am windscreen — runway centerline disappearing into fog

### los_rodeos_runway (target: 8-10 shots)
- Wide ground-level shot of fog rolling across tarmac
- Runway edge lights as halos receding into mist
- Silhouette of the KLM 747 tail fin in fog
- Side view of KLM 747 accelerating with motion blur on engines
- Wide of the KLM 747 silhouette nose-up against fog
- Wide of the orange fireball blooming 150m down the runway
- Wide of burning wreckage in fog at night
- Wide of crash site at dawn three days later
- Investigators walking the debris field in daylight
- Runway edge lights cold and dark three days later

### klm_amsterdam_office (target: 4-5 shots)
- Wide office interior at night with fluorescent lighting
- Close on the rotary telephone on a desk
- Close on the wall map with red pin in Tenerife
- Close on an open staff directory page with Van Zanten's name visible
- Close on two suited hands at edge of frame — one reaching toward phone, one stopping it

### investigation_room (target: 3-4 shots)
- Close on the orange cockpit voice recorder on an evidence tray
- Wide of the examination room with the reel-to-reel and evidence
- Close on the reel-to-reel tape spinning slowly
- Close on a transcript page with handwritten red-pen annotations

### dutch_newspaper (target: 2-3 shots)
- Wide of newspaper lying flat on wooden surface, full front page visible

### archive_room (target: 2 shots)
