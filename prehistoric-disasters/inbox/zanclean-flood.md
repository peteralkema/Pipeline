title: The Zanclean Flood: The Megaflood That Refilled the Mediterranean
description: Five and a half million years ago, the Mediterranean Sea vanished. Cut off from the Atlantic, it dried until it was a sunken desert of salt and brine, almost two miles below the level of the world ocean — the deepest, hottest, deadest place on the face of the Earth. And then, in a single geological instant, the Atlantic broke through. This is the story of the Zanclean Flood: the largest flood in the history of our planet, a waterfall a thousand times the size of the Amazon, refilling an entire sea in a matter of months. Drawn from the salt buried beneath the seabed, the drowned canyons carved into the rock, and the strange chemistry frozen into the stone. If a sea can die and be reborn once, what does that say about the ones we know? Tell us what you believe in the comments.

## PART ONE — The Sea That Died
