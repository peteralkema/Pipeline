## PART TWO - The Green Conquest

[A] The first forests were unlike anything alive today, but they were true trees - tall, woody, and, crucially, deeply rooted. And it was their roots that would change the world.

[A] This was a revolution. For the first time, the continents were being clothed in true soil and true forest. The bare rocky world was turning green, as the trees spread across the land, age after age.

## PART THREE - How a Forest Drowns an Ocean
