This is the story of the Great Dying — the slow, suffocating catastrophe that killed ninety-six percent of life in the sea and seven in ten on the land, not in a single day, but over thousands of years, poisoned from within by the Earth itself. Drawn from the lava that buried a land the size of a continent in Siberia, and from the layer in the rock where, almost everywhere on Earth, the fossils simply stop.
