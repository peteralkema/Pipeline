## PART ONE - THE ONE WHO LOOKED FIRST

[A] To understand Semjaza, begin with what a watcher was. Not a low spirit, but one of the high ones - set over the young world to guard it, to watch it, to keep its order from above. Semjaza stood among the greatest of them.

## PART TWO - THE FEAR OF THE LEADER

[A] But the very next thing the text records is not triumph. It is fear. Semjaza, having proposed the fall, immediately doubts the others will follow him through it. And that doubt reveals everything about him.

[A] I fear, he says to them, that you will not truly go through with this - that you will lose your nerve, and I alone will have to pay the penalty for this great sin. The leader is afraid of being abandoned by the ones he is leading.
