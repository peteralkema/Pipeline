## PART ONE - THE WONDER OVERHEAD

## PART TWO - THE READING OF FATE

[A] What Kokabiel taught, the old text says, was the reading of the signs - to look at the position of the stars and the wheeling of the constellations and to see, written there, the fate of a man, a king, a nation. He taught mankind to divine destiny in the sky.

[A] It is a seductive knowledge, and you can feel why men took it. Who would not want to know what is coming? To read the future in the stars is to feel, for a moment, that you have escaped the terror of not knowing - that the sky itself will tell you what tomorrow holds.

[A] But the gift carries a hidden chain. For if your fate is written in the stars, then you are no longer free. The lights above have already decided. You are not a soul choosing your way, but a prisoner reading your sentence in a sky that cannot be argued with.

## PART THREE - THE SKY INSTEAD OF THE MAKER

[A] But the deepest harm in Kokabiel's gift is not fear. It is a turning away. For to read your fate in the stars is to treat the stars as the power over your life - and that is to bow to the creation instead of the Creator.

[A] The scriptures are severe on exactly this. The stars were made to serve mankind - to give light, to mark time. Kokabiel inverted it. He taught mankind to serve the stars, to fear them, to obey them, to read their commands in the night. The servant lights became false gods.

[A] And this is why the old text counts it among the great crimes, though no blood is spilled. Kokabiel did not teach mankind to kill. He taught them to worship the wrong thing - to give a sky of powerless lights the reverence that belonged to the One who lit them.
