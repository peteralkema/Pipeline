## PART ONE - THE ART THAT SEEMED LIKE MERCY

[A] To feel Armaros's crime, you first have to feel how good it seems. Of all the watchers' gifts, this is the one that wears the kindest face. It does not arm the hand or deceive the heart. It frees. It looses. It undoes what harms.

[A] Imagine a man under a curse, or bound by an oath he made in error, or caught in an enchantment he never chose. And now imagine an angel who can teach him to undo it - to loose the binding, to break the spell, to walk free. Who would call that a crime?

[A] And that is exactly the seduction. The gift of unbinding always arrives disguised as a rescue. It is offered in the name of freedom, of relief, of undoing a wrong. It never announces itself as what it also is - the power to break the good bindings along with the bad.

[A] For the skill does not know the difference. A hand that can loose a curse can loose a covenant. A craft that can break an evil spell can break a holy vow. The undoing does not ask what it undoes. It only undoes - and that indifference is the whole danger.

## PART TWO - THE POWER TO REVERSE

[A] Because think about what a binding is. When something is bound, it is made to hold - fixed, sealed, kept in place against the forces that would pull it apart. A knot holds a rope. A vow holds a promise. A seal holds a thing shut. Binding is how order resists chaos.

[A] And unbinding is the reverse. It is the loosing of what was made to hold. In small things, harmless - a knot untied, a rope coiled away. But raise it to the level of vows and seals and the deep bindings of the world, and unbinding becomes the undoing of order itself.

[A] This is why the old text counts Armaros's gift among the corruptions. He did not teach a harmless trick. He taught mankind the principle of reversal - that what has been bound can be unbound, that what has been sealed can be opened, that no binding need be final if you know the art.

[A] And once a race believes that, something shifts. If every binding can be undone, then no vow is truly binding, no oath truly holds, no seal is truly final. The whole architecture of trust and law and order becomes negotiable - a thing to be loosed by anyone who has learned the skill.

## PART THREE - WHAT SHOULD NEVER BE LOOSED

[A] To teach the art of unbinding is to hand a race a key that fits every lock - including the ones locked for a reason. The same skill that frees a man from a curse could loose a seal set by heaven itself. Not every binding is a prison. Some are a mercy.
