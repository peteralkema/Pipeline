## PART ONE - THE MIXED THING

[A] To understand demons, the old text says, you must first understand what the giants were. And they were not simply large men. They were a category that should never have existed - a joining of two things that were never meant to be joined.

[A] But the old text goes further, and explains something that has haunted every ghost story ever told. Why would such a spirit be hostile? Why would the homeless dead trouble the living? And the answer is a kind of terrible envy.
