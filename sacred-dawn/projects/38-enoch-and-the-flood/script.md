## PART ONE - THE PROPHET OF THE FLOOD

[A] To understand Enoch and the Flood, see him first as its prophet. For Enoch was not only the man who walked with God and journeyed through the heavens. He was the one shown the judgement to come - the Flood that would end the corrupted world.

[A] In his visions, Enoch saw it all. The corruption of the world under the fallen. The cry of the earth rising to heaven. And the judgement fixed in answer - the waters that would drown the giants, cleanse the earth, and end the first age. He was shown the whole shape of the coming Flood.

[A] And he understood what he saw. The Flood was not random ruin. It was judgement - the answer to the fall, the cleansing of a world too corrupt to be reformed. Enoch saw the justice of it, and the mercy folded within it: the world ended, and a remnant carried through to begin again.

[A] So Enoch carried the knowledge of the end before it came. He knew the world was doomed. He knew the waters were coming. He knew the fallen would be judged and the earth cleansed. He walked among a doomed world carrying the vision of its ending. He alone truly saw what was ahead.

## PART THREE - THE ONE WHO WAS TAKEN

[A] And then, before the Flood came, Enoch was taken. Genesis renders it in the simplest and strangest words. Enoch walked with God, and he was not, for God took him. No death. No grave. Simply gone - lifted from the earth by God himself.
