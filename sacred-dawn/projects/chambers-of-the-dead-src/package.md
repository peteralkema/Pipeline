## 3. THE THUMBNAIL

**Status: NOT YET RENDERED. Step 0 is not complete until `thumbnail.png` exists on disk and has
passed the 120px squint test beside Enoch Unsealed's tile.**

### The lane's grammar, observed

Nine of twelve competing tiles in the current Enoch feed run the identical system: **50/50 vertical
split — cinematic scene left, aged open manuscript with a large engraving right — amber and gold on
near-black, one or two words in heavy white flat sans, bottom-left.** Enoch Unsealed established it;
TheBlackParchment cloned the structure and differentiated the dialect (carved stone bas-relief
against Enoch Unsealed's painterly), and is winning with it.

### The conflict with `_Sacred-Dawn.md` §8, stated plainly

Channel doctrine is **one dominating phenomenon + tiny human for scale + lockup in reserved negative
space**, with lockup type matched across videos for series branding. The locked Methuselah tile is
head-and-shoulders on black. **Adopting the split-manuscript panel would break series branding and
make this the fourth channel using the swarm's badge.**

### Decision

**Adopt the palette, refuse the badge.**

- **Amber/bronze on near-black.** Already native — `style_suffix` is *burnished bronze and aged
  stone against deep indigo shadow*. The §8 law is CONTRAST NOT BRIGHTNESS and the lane agrees.
- **One dominating phenomenon:** the mountain of hard rock opened in section, four chamber mouths
  in its face — three lit, the fourth dark and sealed. Tiny witness figure at the bottom of the
  frame (the scale-vs-humanity signature).
- **Lockup:** two lines, matched type to Methuselah — `THE FOUR CHAMBERS` / **`AND THE ONE WITH NO
  WAY OUT`**. Anchor top-left, drop to bottom-left if the subject rides high. Render both, choose,
  fail to top-left.
- **No manuscript panel. No 4K badge.** Both are swarm tells; the render quality is the
  differentiator and a book panel spends half the frame on a container.

### The test that settles it

Ship the above as A. Build the split-panel version as B. **Run Studio's thumbnail test-and-compare.**
The bright-HDR repackaging of batch 2 was shipped across 63 videos with no control and taught us
nothing; this is the cheap way to not repeat that.

**Squint protocol:** render at 1280×720, view at 120px beside Enoch Unsealed's AFTERLIFE tile and
TheBlackParchment's 8 RULES tile. If the four mouths do not read as four at 120px, the concept fails
and the tile becomes a single sealed mouth.
