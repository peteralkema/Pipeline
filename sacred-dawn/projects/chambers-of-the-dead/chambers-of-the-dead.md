title: The Book of Enoch Says Your Soul Waits in One of Four Places — And One Has No Way Out
description: What happens to your soul in the first hours after death? The Book of Enoch — the banned book quoted in the New Testament and found among the Dead Sea Scrolls — records an answer almost no one has read. A man is carried out of the world and west past the rivers of fire, the pillars of heaven, and the prison of the fallen stars, to a hollow mountain at the end of the earth where every soul that has ever lived is waiting in one of four chambers. Three of them open on the appointed day. One of them has no way out. A cinematic recreation of First Enoch chapters seventeen to twenty-two. All supernatural claims are presented as what the ancient texts themselves say.
