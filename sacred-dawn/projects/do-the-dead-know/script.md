description: Whether someone who died can still see you is one of grief's oldest, rawest questions -- and scripture gives a real, honest answer split two ways. Job and Ecclesiastes say the dead know nothing of what happens on earth. This film follows six scenes -- Job, Ecclesiastes, the cloud of witnesses, Moses and Elijah at the Transfiguration, the rich man's warning to his living brothers, and the joy in heaven over one repentant heart -- to find the honest, bounded answer between silence and surveillance.
