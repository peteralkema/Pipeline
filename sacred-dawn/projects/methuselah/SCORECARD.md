### The two Step-1 decisions that caused it
1. **A biographical frame produces a documentary.** "The life of X over 969 years" is mostly
   ordinary life; the spectacle is what he WITNESSES, so a chronological life-frame demotes every
   supernatural event to a cameo. It wanted an **event frame**.
2. **The spine object became a visual sink.** One stone per year forced two rock tokens to **26% of
   the film** — 125 beats of a man putting a stone on a rock.

### What was available and unused
The throne of fire · the storehouses of the stars · the prison of the fallen stars · the chambers of
the dead · the seven mountains · Ohyah and Hahyah's dreams · Mahaway's flight. **Sacred Dawn has
already shipped all of it.** Enoch's bodily ascent into heaven got a cave and some stretched hides.

## C. WHAT THIS MEANS

**The craft did not fail. The measurement did.** Every audit that existed at build time **passed**:
framing mix healthy, verb histogram improved, near-duplicates down 28%, gates green, canon clean,
VO converged. A human reviewing 1,200 stills then named seventeen recurring subjects unprompted —
**and they were the seventeen canon tokens.**

The instruments were measuring the arrangement of the frame and never its contents.

**Diagnose the two layers apart.** Mid-film retention on this channel holds at **0.70–0.75
relative**, so the narrative information rate is working — the story moves. What is flat is what
the viewer looks at. *Retention tells you whether the story moves; the three dials tell you whether
the pictures do.*

**And the competitor's Methuselah — 698K in eleven days — has the same class of visuals.** So
monotony is not what separates 698K from 345 views. This scorecard is about the ceiling, not the
floor: fixing A gets a better film, but it is not the variable that explains the gap. **CTR and the
first sixty seconds still are.**

## D. THE FORCING FUNCTIONS NOW IN PLACE

*None of these existed when this film was built. All are now in `_LEGO.md`.*

| failure | forcing function | when it fires |
|---|---|---|
| A1 spectacle share | **visual budget gate** — % spectacle, single-token cap, does each act own its tokens | Step 1, before authoring |
| A2 novelty | **subject ledger** — subjects allocated to ONE block; spent = **forbidden** | Step 1 allocation, Step 3 audit |
| A3 span | the ledger *is* the clustering | Step 1 |
| A4 escalation | **scale budget** — no `scale` 5 before the final third | Step 1 ration, Step 3 audit |
| A5 container | **`subject` column names the EVENT, not the place** | Step 2 authoring |
| A6 spine range | **ten-frames test** — name ten distinct pictures or it is a sink | Step 1 |

**The economics inversion is the whole point.** Before: reuse was cheap and correct, invention was
expensive and correct, so cheap won 480 times. After: **reuse is illegal**, so invention is the only
legal move.

## E. FOR THE NEXT FILM — THE PASS MARK

A film should not go to grid until:

- **Architecture ≥ 70.** Below that, no amount of craft rescues it — Methuselah is the proof.
- **Novelty ≥ 2 new subjects in every block**, including the last.
- **Span ≤ 0.35** for every subject except one declared spine.
- **No `scale` 5 before the final third.**
- **≥ 50% of beats carry a specific action**, not a place with nothing happening.
- **Spectacle share ≥ 30%** on a blockbuster-register channel.

*Methuselah passes none of these. It ships anyway — the packaging is strong, the VO is clean, and
the comparison case proves the visuals are not the binding constraint. But it is the LAST film
built without the ledger.*
