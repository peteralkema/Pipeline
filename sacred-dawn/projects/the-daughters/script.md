[A] First the herds. A giant could empty a hillside of sheep between dawn and noon, and rise still hungry.

VISUAL: a giant female silhouette in the foreground raising a huge bronze mirror, behind her two giants clashing in firelit dust, the chaos reflected small in the polished disc.

VISUAL: an enormous female silhouette seated on a stone height in firelight, worshippers tiny at the base, a huge bronze mirror resting against her knee throwing back the flames.

## PART SIX — HEAVEN ANSWERS

[A] They saw the stripped fields. The flattened groves. The valleys opened like wounds. They saw the giants, and the daughters of the giants, and the men on their knees.

[A] And they did what the Watchers were supposed to do, and had stopped doing. They carried it upward. They brought the cry of the earth before the throne.
VISUAL: four winged figures rising and turning upward into blinding cold-gold light, wings sweeping down, light flaring out around them until the figures are nearly lost in it.

VISUAL: a single vast shaft of cold light breaking downward through black storm-cloud toward the earth, the cloud peeling back from its edges, the beam widening as it falls.
