title: Why Do Good People Die Young? The Answer Enoch Was Given
description: Why do good people die young, while people who seem to deserve it live on? It is the oldest, angriest question there is -- and scripture puts it directly to an angel, and records the answer. The Book of Enoch says the young dead were not punished, forgotten, or unlucky. They were taken FROM something -- not robbed of something. Wisdom, Isaiah, and the story of a real king named Josiah all make the same specific claim, in almost the same words. This film follows that claim from its oldest complaint to its strangest, most honest answer.
