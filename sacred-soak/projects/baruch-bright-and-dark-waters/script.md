## PART TWENTY - The Last Rest

[A] And at the last, I wrote to them, said Baruch, after all the waters, dark and bright, and after the harvest, and the gathering, there is a rest, a deep and gentle rest, for all the faithful, where the turning is ended, and the sorrow is no more, and the bright water stays, and does not pass, and there is only peace, and light, and gladness, without end.
VISUAL: A wide earth of still shining waters under a soft warm light, a deep and boundless calm, awe-filled and serene, no figures.

[A] And into that rest, I wrote, said Baruch, all who have kept their hope through the dark waters shall come, and lay down their long weariness, and be at peace, and the scattered shall be gathered, and the grieving comforted, and the far brought home, and there shall be no more dark water, ever, but only the still bright water, and the gentle light, for ever.
VISUAL: A wide earth of still shining waters under a soft warm light, a deep and endless peace, awe-filled and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the last rest be the comfort you carry down, said the night - the deep gentle rest after all the waters, the turning ended, the sorrow no more, the bright water staying, the weary laying down their weariness, the scattered gathered, the far brought home, only light and gladness for ever. There is a last rest, deep and gentle, waiting. Rest toward it, gently, your long weariness soon laid down, in the still bright water.

## PART TWENTY-ONE - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have watched the vision of Baruch - the cloud, and the waters dark and bright, the turning of the ages, the last dark water and the healing lightning, the brightest waters that come last and stay, and the letter of comfort to the scattered, that they too might hope, and be gathered, and rest.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the cloud, or the waters, or the letter. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old vision rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The ages turn, dark and bright, and your own days turn with them, but neither the dark endures nor is it the end; the brightest waters come last, and stay; and if you feel far, or scattered, or alone, you are not forgotten, but held, and your gathering is coming. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. Whatever water today was, it is passing, and held, and turning toward the brightest water of all, that comes at the last and stays. You are not forgotten, nor hidden, nor left behind. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, rest in the light that comes last. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
