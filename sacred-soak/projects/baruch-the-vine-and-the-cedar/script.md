## PART TWENTY - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have been shown, with Baruch, the vine and the cedar - the great proud forest, the small gentle vine, the quiet fountain, the rising waters, the forest uprooted, the last cedar brought down, the vine remaining, and the dawn breaking, soft and golden, over the cleared and peaceful plain.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the forest, or the vine, or the fountain. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old vision rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The proud powers that seem so permanent are only a passing forest; the gentle things that seem so frail are the enduring vine; the fountain of peace within you does not run dry; and whatever presses you now shall pass, and the vine shall grow where it stood. Do not fear the proud. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. The forests that loom over your life are only a season; the gentle vine endures; the quiet fountain flows on within you; and the dawn is coming, soft and golden, over a cleared and peaceful plain. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, do not fear the proud, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
