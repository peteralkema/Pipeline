## PART TWELVE — Closing Reflection

[A] So ends the Book of Enoch — the watchers who fell, the throne of fire, the parables and the Son of Man, the secrets of the lights, the dream of all history, and these last words of comfort: the ages of the world, the two ways, the bright child named, and the blessing kept for the faithful. The whole of the old book, read for rest, is closed.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning lower now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the ten ages, or the count of the weeks, or the names of the angels and the stars. It was all set down long ago by a scribe who walked with God, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old words rest where they have fallen, and let the night be still. However long the ages, the light came. However dark the weeks, a remnant was kept. However weary the road, the promise held — that those who sleep in righteousness shall wake into a great light, and shine as the lights of heaven. Rest in that promise, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You are kept, and remembered, and held in a promise older than the stars — a comfort named in the dark, a light that does not end, a rest prepared for all who keep the way. And be at peace. The old book is closed, and the night is long, and you are safe within it. Until then — rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
