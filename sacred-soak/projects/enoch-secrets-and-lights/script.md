## PART NINETEEN — Closing Reflection

[A] So ends this long soaking in the old words — the resting of the righteous, the secrets of the lightning and the thunder, the day the kings behold the Son of Man, the hidden name that founded the heaven, the scribe carried home, and the great slow calendar of the lights, gate by gate, season by season.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning lower now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the courses of the sun, or the measure of the moon, or the names of the gates of the wind. They were set down long ago by a scribe who walked with God, that they might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the words rest where they have fallen, and let the night be still. The sun keeps his portals. The moon keeps her measure. VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] You are held by the same order that holds the sun and the moon and the stars, said the night, and it does not fail, and it does not sleep, though you may. The same hidden word that binds the sea and turns the lights is laid quietly over you. You need not keep it. You need only rest within it.
VISUAL: A wide still dark sea beneath a vast calm sky, the surface smooth and patient and bounded, the faint light of dawn held far off, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. Let the throne, and the judgement, and the flood, and the long calendar of the lights all settle softly behind you, and keep only the peace that was offered first, and kept for last. And be at peace, until we open the old book again. Until then — rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
