## PART TWENTY-ONE - The Souls Yet to Be

[A] And Metatron showed me, said the sage, the chamber of the souls yet to be born, the spirits waiting their time, kept gently in the light, each known, each named, each appointed a day to come into the world, and I saw that no soul comes by chance, but each is known before it is born, and loved, and awaited.

[A] And each of them, said the sage, is precious, before ever it has done anything, or become anything, loved for itself, known by name, and kept, and sent forth in its time, into the world, to live, and to seek, and to come home again at the last; and so every soul that is, was first a soul that was known, and awaited, and loved.

[A] And when I had seen all these things, said the sage, Metatron laid his hand upon me gently, and blessed me, and said: go now, in peace, and carry what you have seen, that the faithful may be comforted, and know the order in which the world is kept, and the mercy with which it is judged, and the peace that is offered to all.

[A] So tonight we have gone deeper with the sage, into the chambers of heaven - the sun and the moon, the winds and the rain, the thunder kept gently, the pillars of the world, the bounds of the sea, the watchers of the nations, the veil of the ages, the court of mercy, the book of life, and the measure of peace that holds all things.
