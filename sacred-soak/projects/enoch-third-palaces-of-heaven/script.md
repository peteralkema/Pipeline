VISUAL: A soft luminous spring of gentle light rising in a still court, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-ONE - The Righteous at Rest

[A] And Metatron showed me, said the sage, in the courts of light, the righteous who had gone before, the faithful of all the ages, at rest, and glad, clothed in soft light, and I saw the fathers, and the prophets, and the gentle ones, and the merciful, and the humble, all gathered in the palaces, in peace, waiting for the great day of the gathering.

## PART TWENTY-TWO - The Book of Remembrance

[A] And Metatron showed me, said the sage, the book of remembrance, in which are written the deeds of men, and I feared, but he said: do not fear, for the Most High writes in mercy, and remembers the good, and the smallest kindness is set down, and not forgotten, and the tears of the sorrowful are counted, and the patience of the weary is kept, and none of it is lost.

[A] And over all the palaces, said the sage, there lay a great peace, a rest, a sabbath, and Metatron said: this is the rest of the Most High, the peace that he keeps, and gives, and the bright ones keep it with him, and it flows down from the throne, through all the courts, and out, gently, to the earth, to all who will receive it.
