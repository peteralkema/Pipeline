[A] But they were not lost to the Most High, said Ezra, for he had led them there, that they might keep the law in quietness, where no nation troubled them, and there they dwelt, faithful, in the far land, remembering the Lord, and keeping his ways, and waiting, through the long years, for the day they should be brought home.
VISUAL: A wide remote land at dusk under a soft sky, faint distant dwellings touched with a gentle warm light, a quiet faithfulness in the far place, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the tribes led far away be gentle in your sleep, said the night - the people led to the ends of the earth, lost to the sight of men, but never lost to the Most High, kept faithful in the far land, remembering, and waiting to be brought home. Even those who seem most lost, and most far, are not lost to God, but kept, and waited for. Rest in that, that nothing faithful is ever truly lost.
VISUAL: A wide remote land at dusk, faint distant dwellings in a gentle warm light, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-ONE - The Long Journey Home

[A] And in the last days, said the Most High, I will bring them home again, from the far country, and they shall journey back, the long way, through the region of the great river, and I will hold back the waters for them, as I held them back of old, that they may cross over, and come home, to the land, and to the gathering.
VISUAL: A wide dim land at first light, a great calm river drawn gently back to make a soft path, a faint sense of a long homeward way opening, dreamlike and reverent, no figures.

[A] And they shall come home in peace, said the Most High, gently, and gladly, gathered from the ends of the earth, the lost brought back, the scattered gathered, the far-off drawn near, and they shall stand with the deliverer, in the light, and there shall be no more exile, and no more distance, but only the gathering, and the home, and the peace.
VISUAL: A wide land at soft dawn, a gentle homeward path of warm light winding across it toward a distant brightness, a deep and welcoming peace, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the long journey home be the comfort in your sleep, said the night - the lost tribes brought back from the far country, the waters held gently back, the long road home opening, the scattered gathered, the far-off drawn near, no more exile, only the gathering. However far you feel, there is a road home, and it is being opened. Rest in that, on the homeward way, gently drawn near.
VISUAL: A wide land at soft dawn, a gentle homeward path of warm light toward a distant brightness, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-TWO - The Cup of Understanding

[A] And I remember, said Ezra, the cup that I was given to drink, in the quiet field, when the Most High kindled understanding in me - it was like water, but the colour of fire, and when I drank it, my heart poured forth wisdom, and my breast gave out understanding, and my spirit kept its memory, and the deep things were opened to me.
VISUAL: A wide quiet field at dawn, a soft warm glow gathering gently over a faint seated figure as of understanding poured out, a tender radiance, reverent and serene, no faces shown.

[A] And the wisdom did not fail, said Ezra, all the forty days, but flowed, and flowed, like a spring that does not run dry, and the more I gave out, the more was given, and I understood that the springs of understanding are in the Most High, and are without end, and are given freely to those who seek them, and thirst for them.
VISUAL: A soft gentle spring of pale luminous water rising quietly in a still dim place, flowing softly and without end, a deep and gentle constancy, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the cup of understanding be gentle in your sleep, said the night - the water the colour of fire, the wisdom poured out, the spring that does not run dry, the more given the more that comes, the springs of understanding without end in the Most High. There is a spring of understanding that never fails, given to the seeking heart. Rest beside it, gently, where the wisdom flows and does not run dry.
VISUAL: A soft gentle spring of pale luminous water rising quietly in a still place, flowing softly, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-THREE - The Fountain for the Seeking Heart

[A] For the springs of wisdom, said Ezra, are kept for those who seek them, and the fountain of understanding for those who thirst, and the streams of knowledge for those who love the truth, and they are not given to the proud, nor to the hurried, but to the humble, and the patient, and the seeking, who come to them gently, and drink, and are filled.
VISUAL: A soft luminous spring in a still dim garden, gentle streams flowing quietly from it, a deep and welcoming calm, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] And whoever drinks of them, said Ezra, shall not thirst again, but shall be satisfied, and shall have light in the heart, and understanding in the breast, and peace in the spirit, and shall walk in the truth, and not stumble, and shall be at rest, for the springs of the Most High do not fail, but flow, for ever, for all who seek them.
VISUAL: A soft luminous spring in a still garden, gentle streams flowing quietly, a soft warm light over the water, a deep and satisfied peace, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the fountain for the seeking heart be the thought you carry down, said the night - the springs of wisdom kept for the humble and the patient and the seeking, the fountain that does not fail, that satisfies, and gives light, and understanding, and peace. Come gently to the springs, tonight, humble and seeking, and drink, and be filled, and be at rest. Rest beside the fountain that does not fail.
VISUAL: A soft luminous spring in a still garden, gentle streams flowing quietly, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-FOUR - The Word for the Generations

[A] And all these things, said Ezra, the open books and the secret, the visions and the wisdom, were set down, and kept, that the generations to come might have them, and be comforted, and know that the hard powers pass, and the deliverer comes, and the lost are gathered, and the springs of wisdom do not fail, and the light is not lost.
VISUAL: Soft pale books of gentle light resting in a quiet place at dawn, faint and luminous, a warm glow upon them, a sense of words kept for the ages, reverent and serene, no figures.

[A] For the sorrow of the present is short, said Ezra, and the glory to come is long; the powers that trouble the world are passing; the deliverer is near; and the springs of understanding are open to all who seek them. Be comforted, therefore, you who hear these words, in whatever age you hear them, and hope, and do not be afraid.
VISUAL: A wide quiet land at soft dawn, a gentle warm light spreading over it, a deep and hopeful calm, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the word for the generations be gentle in your sleep, said the night - the visions and the wisdom set down and kept, that whoever hears them, in whatever age, might be comforted: the hard powers pass, the deliverer comes, the lost are gathered, the springs do not fail, the light is not lost. You are the generation that hears it now. Rest in that comfort, kept and given, across all the years, for you.

## PART TWENTY-FIVE - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have seen Ezra's later visions - the great eagle that rose and reigned and passed away, the man from the sea who gathered the lost with a word, and the restoring of the sacred books over forty days, and the taking of the prophet, and the word that remains, and the light carried down to us.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the eagle, or the deliverer, or the restored books. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old visions rest where they have fallen, and let the night be still. The hard powers rise and pass like wings upon an eagle; the deliverer gathers the lost with a word, not a sword; and the light, once restored, is carried down, hand to hand, all the long way, to you. The hard things pass, and the good things come, and the light reaches you. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. The powers that trouble the world are passing things; the deliverer is near; the gathering is coming; and the light has been carried all this way, for you. Another night we will rise with an old sage through the palaces of heaven. But for now, lay down your questions, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
