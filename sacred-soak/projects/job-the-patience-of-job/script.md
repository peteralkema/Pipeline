## PART TWENTY-ONE - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have heard the testament of Job - his fullness, his testing, the patience he kept, the hope that held him, the restoration, the open house, the faithful wife, the shining cords, the daughters singing in the tongue of angels, and the gentle carrying-home in a chariot of light.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the testing, or the restoration, or the songs. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old testament rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The darkness does not last; patience is a daily holding-on; the truest comfort is presence, not answers; the Redeemer lives; and at the last there is a gentle carrying-home, in a chariot of light, amid singing. Whatever you are enduring, it will not have the last word. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You will be kept through whatever testing, as Job was kept, and restored, and carried home at the last in light, amid singing. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, endure gently, and rest. Until then - rest.
