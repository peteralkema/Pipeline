[A] So tonight we have heard the testament of Joseph - his long suffering borne without bitterness, his faith in the low place, the raising up, the not-avenging, the covering of the wrong, the weeping and the reconciliation, the God who meant it for good, the freedom of forgiveness, and the passing in peace, in the hope of home.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the suffering, or the forgiveness, or the reconciliation. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old story rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. Bitterness is a second wound we give ourselves; forgiveness sets free the one who forgives; love covers a multitude of wrongs; and the Lord can weave even the wrongs done to us into good, so that nothing is wasted. Whatever was done to you, you may lay down the bitterness, and be free. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You may lay down every bitterness tonight, and keep your heart soft, and be free, for the Lord weaves even the wrongs done to you into good, and nothing is wasted, and the end of it is blessing. Another night we will sit with Abraham at the oak, in his gentle farewell. But for now, forgive, and be free, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
