[A] So tonight we have crossed with Jacob to the reconciliation - the fearful reunion, the weight of the old wrong, the gifts sent ahead, the prayer in the night, the wrestling in the dark, the holding-on, the new name, the wound that was a blessing, the breaking of the day, and the brother who ran, not to avenge, but to embrace.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the fear, or the wrestling, or the reconciliation. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old story rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The dark wrestlings, held to the end, yield a blessing; the wounds we carry may be signs of blessing; the fear is often larger than the danger; and the meeting we dread may run to embrace us, weeping, and glad. Lay down your fears at the ford, and rest. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. Hold on through your dark wrestlings, for they will bless you; and do not dread your feared meetings overmuch, for the mercy is often waiting where you feared a reckoning, and the brother may be running to embrace you. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, lay down your fear at the ford, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
