VISUAL: A wide quiet land at night, a faint resting form under a great luminous ladder and soft stars, a warm enfolding radiance about it, awe-filled and serene, no faces shown.

[A] For you do not lie down alone, says the night, however lonely the place, for the gate of heaven is open above you, and the light comes down to where you are, and the God of the highest heaven is near to you, and keeps you, and goes with you, on all your roads, and will bring you, at the last, home; and so you may rest, tonight, as Jacob rested, comforted, and no longer alone.
VISUAL: A wide quiet land at night, a faint resting form under a luminous ladder and soft stars, a deep and enfolding peace, awe-filled and reverent, no faces shown.

[A] Let the I-am-with-you-wherever-you-go be the last gentle thought of your night, said the night - the promise given still to you, over your own pillow: I am with you, and will keep you, and will not leave you, and will bring you home. You do not lie down alone tonight. Rest in that, gently, the gate open above you, the light coming down, God near, keeping you, going with you, bringing you home.
VISUAL: A wide quiet land at night, a faint resting form under a luminous ladder and soft stars, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no faces shown.

## PART TWENTY - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have lain down with Jacob in the lonely place - the setting-out alone, the stone for a pillow, the open stars, the ladder joining earth and heaven, the angels ascending and descending, the Lord above the stair, and the gentle promise: I am with you, and will keep you, and will bring you home.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the ladder, or the promise, or the vow. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old dream rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The place you thought empty may be full of God; the gate of heaven stands open above your very pillow; and to every lonely traveler who lies down trusting, the promise is still spoken: I am with you, and will keep you, wherever you go, and will bring you home. You do not lie down alone. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. The ladder still stands above your pillow, the light still comes down to where you lie, and the God of the highest heaven is with you, and will keep you, wherever you go, and will bring you home. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, you are not alone. Rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
