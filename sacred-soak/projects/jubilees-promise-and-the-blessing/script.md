## PART TWENTY-ONE - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have heard a gentle story of a promise kept - the word under the stars, the gentle laughter, the child of the promise, the going up in trust, the God who provides, the staying voice, the great blessing, and the covenant handed down, all the long way, to us.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the promise, or the mountain, or the blessing. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old story rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. The promises that seem most impossible are gently kept, in their own time; the mercy is nearest when the trial seems darkest; and on the mountain, in the hour of need, it is always, gently, provided. You are held by the God who provides, and blessed among the countless stars of the promise. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. The God who provided on the mountain provides for you, faithful, and merciful, just when you have need, and blesses you, countless as the stars. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, rest in the God who provides. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
