Lay down your failings, and rest. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. And a restoration is coming, and a reign of peace, and a gentle waking, for all who sleep. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, lay it down, and rest. Until then - rest.
