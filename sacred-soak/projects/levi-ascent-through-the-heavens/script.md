## PART TWENTY-ONE - The Blessing to the Children

[A] And when Levi was old, says the book, he called his children, and blessed them, and told them the vision, and said: heaven is near to you, and open, and the Most High is gentle, and calls, and welcomes; lift up your hearts to him, and your prayers shall be borne up, and mercy shall be asked for you, and a great peace shall be given you, deeper than any trouble.
VISUAL: A dim warm chamber, a faint older figure with hands raised gently over faint bowed forms, a tender warm light over the blessing, intimate and reverent, no faces shown.

[A] Serve gently, said Levi, and carry mercy, and pour out the graces you are given, and do not lose heart in the troubles of the world, for heaven intercedes for the earth, and the light is coming, and the peace is offered, and you are watched over, and loved, and called; and he blessed them, and they received it, gladly.
VISUAL: A dim warm chamber, a tender warm light over a gentle blessing, faint bowed forms, intimate and reverent, no faces shown.

[A] Let the blessing to the children rest gently on you, said the night - heaven near and open, the Most High gentle and calling, the prayers borne up, mercy asked, a great peace given, deeper than any trouble. Let that blessing fall on you too, tonight: lift your heart, be watched over, be at peace. Rest beneath it, blessed, called, and loved, a great peace given you, deeper than any trouble.
VISUAL: A dim warm chamber, a tender warm light over a gentle blessing, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no faces shown.

## PART TWENTY-TWO - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have risen with Levi through the heavens - the shepherd's grief and prayer, the mountain and the opened heavens, the storehouses kept, the angels who make propitiation, the prayers borne up, the Great Glory, the calling, the washing and the robe of light, the seven graces, and the light carried down.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the heavens, or the robe, or the graces. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old vision rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. Heaven is near, and open to the seeking heart; the hard things are held, and mercy is asked; your prayers, even the wordless ones, are borne up and heard; and a great peace is offered you, deeper than any trouble, if you will only lift your heart and receive it. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. Heaven is near to you, and open, and gentle, and even now your prayers are borne up, and mercy is asked for you, and a great peace is offered, deeper than any trouble of the world. Another night we will sit with Ezra through the seven nights of comfort. But for now, lift your heart gently, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
