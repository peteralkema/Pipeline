VISUAL: A soft warm light spreading gently over a wide still land at dawn, reaching every part, painterly and serene, no figures.

[A] For he does not measure his grace, sang the singer, nor ration it, nor keep it back from any, but pours it out, freely, abundantly, more than can be received, and there is always more, and it never runs low, and whoever turns to it finds it already there, already poured, already waiting, for them.
VISUAL: A soft warm light spreading abundantly over a wide still land at dawn, a deep and generous radiance, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the grace poured on all be gentle in your sleep, said the night - grace poured not on the worthy only but on all, near and far, great and small, freely, abundantly, more than can be received, never running low, already waiting for whoever turns to it. It is already poured out for you, waiting. Rest in that abundance, grace already there, already yours, more than you could ever need.
VISUAL: A soft warm light spreading abundantly over a wide still land at dawn, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-TWO - The Nearness Day and Night

[A] The Lord is near, sang the singer, in the day and in the night, in the waking and in the sleeping, and he does not leave, nor turn away, nor grow distant, but is always at hand, closer than my own breath, and I need not call him from far, for he is already here, already near, already with me, always.
VISUAL: A soft warm radiance close and gentle over a still quiet place, a deep and intimate nearness, painterly and serene, no figures.

[A] And in the night especially, sang the singer, he is near, when the world is quiet, and the striving is laid down, and the heart is still, then his nearness is most felt, gently, like a presence beside the bed, keeping watch, and I am not alone in the dark, but companied, and kept, and held.
VISUAL: A soft warm radiance close and gentle in a still dim place, a tender keeping presence, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the nearness day and night be the comfort in your sleep, said the night - the Lord near in the day and the night, in the waking and the sleeping, closer than your own breath, most felt in the quiet, a presence keeping watch beside you. You are not alone in the dark tonight, but companied, and kept. Rest in that nearness, held, watched over, never alone.
VISUAL: A soft warm radiance close and gentle in a still dim place, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-THREE - The Gladness of the Morning

[A] And in the morning, sang the singer, the redeemed wake glad, for the night is passed, and the light has come, and the sorrow that lingered in the darkness is gone, and joy comes with the morning, and they rise, refreshed, and grateful, and sing, for weeping may endure for a night, but gladness comes with the dawn.
VISUAL: A soft warm dawn breaking gently over a still gentle land, a fresh glad light spreading, painterly and serene, no figures.

[A] And so I lay me down in hope, sang the singer, knowing that the night is not for ever, and the dawn is coming, and the gladness with it, and whatever sorrow the darkness holds, it will pass, and the morning will come, gentle, and glad, and I will wake refreshed, and the light will be there, waiting.
VISUAL: A soft warm dawn breaking over a still land, a fresh and hopeful light, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] Let the gladness of the morning be the last gentle thought of your night, said the night - the redeemed waking glad, the night passed, the sorrow gone, joy coming with the dawn, weeping enduring but a night, gladness coming with the morning. Lie down in that hope: the night is not for ever, the dawn is coming, and gladness with it. Rest, and wake refreshed, into a gentle and glad morning.
VISUAL: A soft warm dawn breaking over a still gentle land, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY-FOUR - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have heard more of the songs of the beloved - the right hand that holds and does not let go, the way made across the rushing waters, the arms lifted and the heaviness fallen, the opened doors and loosed chains, the Lord who is a sun with no more darkness or death, and the going-down to gather the lost, that none be left behind.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the songs, nor even to remember them. They were sung long ago by a heart that had crossed the deep and been carried home, and kept, that they might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old songs rest where they have fallen, and let the night be still. You are held by a hand that does not let go; a way is made for you across every deep; the doors are opened, and the chains loosed; and there is no one so lost that he will not go down to find them, and carry them home. You are sought, and held, and carried. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You are held by the right hand of the one who loves you, carried home at his own gentle pace, resting even as you journey. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, be carried, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
