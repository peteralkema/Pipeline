## PART ONE — The Blessing of Enoch

It is the account of the angels who fell, set down by a scribe named Enoch, so that it would never be forgotten by the generations who came after.

So breathe out, let the day go from your shoulders, and let the old voice begin where the old book begins.

[A] It begins not with the fall, but with a blessing. These are the words of the blessing of Enoch, with which he blessed the chosen and the righteous, who would be living in the day of trouble, when all the wicked and the godless were to be removed from the earth.

## PART TWO — The Order of the Heavens

VISUAL: The interior of an immense hall whose floor and ceiling are alike living fire, the path of the stars wheeling slowly above, the whole space radiant beyond description, awe-filled, no figures.

[A] And I looked, and I saw therein a lofty throne. And its appearance was as crystal, and its wheels were as the shining sun, and there was the vision of cherubim. And from underneath the throne came streams of flaming fire, so that I could not look upon it.
VISUAL: A vast and lofty throne of crystal and pale gold light, streams of soft flaming fire flowing out from beneath it, the brightness too great to look upon directly, immense and reverent, no figure upon it shown, only light.

[A] And the Great Glory sat thereupon. And his raiment shone more brightly than the sun, and was whiter than any snow. None of the angels could enter, and could behold his face, by reason of the magnificence and the glory. And no flesh could behold him.
VISUAL: An overwhelming radiance upon the great throne, brighter than the sun and whiter than snow, the form within it never resolved, only light upon light, faint presences kept back at a great distance, awe-filled and reverent.

[A] A flaming fire was round about him, and a great fire stood before him. And none around him could draw near unto him. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him, yet he needed no counsellor. And the most holy ones who were near to him did not leave by night, nor depart from him.
VISUAL: The vast throne of light surrounded by a great ring of soft fire, countless faint luminous presences arrayed at a great distance around it, none drawing near, the scale beyond counting, reverent and overwhelming, no faces.

[A] And until then I had been prostrate on my face, trembling. And the Lord called me with his own mouth, and said to me: come hither, Enoch, and hear my word. And one of the holy ones came to me, and raised me up, and brought me near to the door. And I bowed my head down.
VISUAL: A small robed figure raised gently from the fiery floor by a faint luminous presence, brought toward the great threshold of light, his head bowed low, the throne radiant beyond, reverent and intimate, faceless.

## PART TEN — The Verdict on the Watchers

[A] And the Lord said unto me: fear not, Enoch, you righteous man, and scribe of righteousness. Approach hither, and hear my voice. And go, say to the watchers of heaven, who have sent you to intercede for them: you should intercede for men, and not men for you.
VISUAL: The small bowed scribe at the threshold of the great throne-light, the radiance spilling gently over him, a sense of being spoken to with both severity and tenderness, reverent and intimate, faceless.

[A] Wherefore have you left the high and holy and eternal heaven, and lain with women, and defiled yourselves with the daughters of men, and taken to yourselves wives, and done as the children of earth, and begotten giants as your sons?
VISUAL: A vast view downward from the heights, the dim earth far below with its long shadows, seen as if from the throne itself, grave and sorrowful, the distance immense, no faces.

[A] Though you were holy, and spiritual, living the eternal life, you have defiled yourselves with the blood of women, and have begotten children with the blood of flesh, and have lusted after the blood of men, and produced flesh and blood, as those do who die and perish.
VISUAL: The gathered shadowed watchers far below the heights, their forms dimmed and bowed, the memory of their lost brightness faint upon them, grave and sorrowful, no faces.

[A] Therefore have I given them wives also, that they might impregnate them, and beget children by them, that thus nothing might be wanting to them on earth. But you were formerly spiritual, living the eternal and immortal life, for all the generations of the world.
VISUAL: A faint vision of the watchers as they once were, luminous and high among the stars, dissolving into their fallen and dimmed forms below, a great glory remembered and lost, reverent and grave, faceless.

[A] And therefore I have not appointed wives for you, for the dwelling of the spiritual ones is in heaven. And now, the giants, who are produced from the spirits and flesh, shall be called evil spirits upon the earth, and on the earth shall be their dwelling.
VISUAL: A wide darkened earth at dusk, faint formless shadows rising from a barren field and dispersing across the land like mist, a sense of restless spirits loosed upon the world, grave and still, no figures.

[A] Evil spirits have proceeded from their bodies, because they are born from men, and from the holy watchers is their beginning and primal origin. They shall be evil spirits upon the earth, and evil spirits shall they be called.
VISUAL: Thin drifting shadows moving low over a grey and silent land beneath a dim sky, formless and restless, scattering into the dusk, grave and quiet, no figures, only the wandering dark.

[A] And the spirits of the giants afflict, oppress, destroy, attack, do battle, and work destruction on the earth, and cause trouble. They take no food, but nevertheless hunger and thirst, and cause offences. And these spirits shall rise up against the children of men, and against the women, because they have proceeded from them.
VISUAL: A dim and troubled land at night, faint formless shadows moving among the small lights of human dwellings, a restless unease over all of it, grave and still, no resolved figures.

[A] So the petition of the watchers was answered, but not with the answer they had hoped for. There would be no forgiveness, and no peace. The scribe had been carried to the very throne of fire, and had heard the verdict from the mouth of the Great Glory itself. And there was nothing left to be said.
VISUAL: The small robed scribe descending gently from the great brightness, carried back down through luminous cloud, the throne-light receding above him, a great weight of sorrow and awe upon him, faceless.

## PART ELEVEN — The First Journey

[A] And the watchers' verdict was spoken, and could not be unspoken. But the scribe's vision did not end at the throne. For having been carried up into heaven, Enoch was now carried further still, out to the hidden places at the ends of the earth, and shown the secrets that are kept there.
VISUAL: A robed figure carried gently through vast luminous cloud toward a distant dark horizon, the throne-light receding behind him, a great journey beginning, weightless and reverent, faceless, from behind.

[A] And they took me, said Enoch, to a place where those who were there were like flaming fire, and when they wished, they appeared as men. And they brought me to the place of darkness, and to a mountain, the point of whose summit reached to heaven.
VISUAL: A colossal mountain rising out of deep shadow, its summit lost in a luminous height far above, faint fiery presences moving at its base, immense and awe-filled, no resolved figures.

[A] And I saw the places of the luminaries, and the treasuries of the stars, and of the thunder, and in the uttermost depths, where were a fiery bow, and arrows in their quiver, and a fiery sword, and all the lightnings.
VISUAL: A vast cavernous height filled with soft points of stored starlight, faint arcs of held lightning gleaming in the dark, a great treasury of the heavens, awe-filled and still, no figures.

[A] And they took me to the living waters, and to the fire of the west, which receives every setting of the sun. And I came to a river of fire, in which the fire flows like water, and discharges itself into the great sea, towards the west.
VISUAL: A wide slow river of soft golden fire flowing through a dark and barren land toward a distant sea, the flame moving like water, immense and strange, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] And I saw the great rivers, and came to the great darkness, and went to where no flesh walks. And I saw the mountains of the darkness of winter, and the place whence all the waters of the deep flow.
VISUAL: A vast dark land of frozen mountains under a colourless sky, deep shadow gathered in the valleys, an immense and lonely cold, no figures, only the silent winter heights.

[A] And I saw the mouths of all the rivers of the earth, and the mouth of the deep. And I saw a deep abyss, with columns of heavenly fire, and among them I saw columns of fire fall, which were beyond measure both toward the height and toward the depth.
VISUAL: A bottomless dark chasm crossed by immense columns of soft falling fire, rising and descending beyond sight, the scale overwhelming, awe-filled and grave, no figures, only the burning depths.

[A] And beyond that abyss I saw a place which had no firmament of the heaven above, and no firmly founded earth beneath it. There was no water upon it, and no birds, but it was a waste and horrible place.
VISUAL: A vast empty void of grey nothingness, no sky above and no ground below, a formless and desolate waste, immense and still, deeply lonely, no figures, only the emptiness.

[A] And there I saw seven stars, like great burning mountains, and like spirits, appealing to me. And the angel said: this place is the end of heaven and earth. This is the prison for the stars of heaven, and for the host of heaven.
VISUAL: Seven great burning points of light bound together in the void like mountains of fire, faint and appealing in the vast dark, immense and sorrowful, no figures, only the imprisoned lights.

[A] And the stars which roll over the fire, said the angel, are those which have transgressed the commandment of God, in the beginning of their rising, because they did not come forth at their appointed times. And he was wroth with them, and bound them, until the time of the consummation of their sin, in the year of the mystery.
VISUAL: The seven bound lights wreathed in slow soft fire in the boundless dark, held fast and waiting, a long sealing for an age beyond counting, grave and reverent, no figures.

[A] And the scribe, who had begged forgiveness for the watchers and been refused, now saw with his own eyes the prison kept for those who break the order of heaven — even the very stars, even the lights of the sky, bound for leaving their appointed way. The lesson of the whole book, shown once more in fire.
VISUAL: The vast void with its seven bound and burning lights, a small distant robed figure beholding them from the edge of the dark, the scale immense around him, awe-filled and grave, faceless.

## PART TWELVE — The Mountain of the Dead

[A] And from the prison of the fallen stars, the angel carried the scribe onward. And I proceeded, said Enoch, to where things were chaotic. And I saw there something horrible. I saw neither a heaven above, nor a firmly founded earth, but a place chaotic and horrible.
VISUAL: A vast formless grey waste under a void without sky, deep shadow gathered in the emptiness, immense and desolate, a place beyond the edge of the made world, no figures, only the chaos.

[A] And there I saw seven stars of heaven bound together in it, like great mountains, and burning with fire. And I asked: for what sin are they bound, and on what account have they been cast in hither? And the angel said: these are of the number of the stars of heaven, which have transgressed the commandment of the Lord, and are bound here until ten thousand years are consummated.
VISUAL: Seven great burning points of light bound together in the dark void like mountains of fire, held fast in the emptiness, immense and sorrowful, no figures, only the imprisoned lights.

[A] And from thence I went to another place, more horrible than the former. And I saw a great fire there, which burnt and blazed, and the place was cleft as far as the abyss, full of great descending columns of fire. Its extent I could not see, neither could I conjecture.
VISUAL: A vast chasm of blazing fire descending beyond sight, immense columns of flame falling into a bottomless dark, the scale overwhelming, awe-filled and grave, no figures, only the burning deep.

[A] Then I said: how fearful is this place, and how terrible to look upon. And the angel answered: this place is the prison of the angels, and here they shall be held for ever. So even in the journey, the lesson returns. The order of heaven is kept, and the breaking of it is kept also, sealed in fire against the day.
VISUAL: The vast fiery chasm seen from its dark edge, a small distant robed figure beholding it, the burning depths immense around him, awe-filled and grave, faceless.

[A] And from thence I went to another place, and he showed me in the west a great and high mountain, and a hard rock, and four hollow places in it, deep and wide and very smooth. How smooth are these hollow places, and deep and dark to look upon.
VISUAL: A great pale mountain of hard rock rising in the western dark, four deep smooth hollows cut into its side, immense and still, a quiet and solemn place, no figures, only the mountain and its chambers.

[A] And the angel answered: these hollow places have been created for this very purpose, that the spirits of the souls of the dead should assemble in them. Here their souls are gathered, and these places are made to receive them, until the day of their judgement, and until their appointed period.
VISUAL: The four deep chambers within the pale mountain, a soft and quiet dimness within them, a sense of a vast waiting stillness, reverent and peaceful, no figures, only the resting places.

[A] And the period is long, until the great judgement comes upon them. And I saw the spirits of the children of men who were dead, and their voice went forth to heaven, and made suit. So even the dead are kept, and remembered, and their cry is not lost, but waits, gathered in the mountain, until the day appointed.
VISUAL: The pale mountain of the dead under a still grey sky, a faint soft light within its hollow chambers, a great and patient quiet over all of it, reverent and peaceful, no figures.

## PART THIRTEEN — The Tree of Life

[A] And the journey turned at last toward beauty. And from thence I went to another place, toward the west, to the ends of the earth. And I saw a burning fire which ran without resting, and did not cease from its course by day or by night, but ran regularly.
VISUAL: A wide land at the western ends of the earth, a steady ribbon of soft golden fire running unbroken across it beneath a vast sky, calm and ceaseless, painterly and reverent, no figures.

[A] And I saw seven magnificent mountains, all differing each from the other. And their stones were magnificent and beautiful, magnificent as a whole, of glorious appearance and fair exterior. Three mountains toward the east, and three toward the south. And there were deep ravines among them, and none drew near to the others.
VISUAL: Seven great and beautiful mountains arrayed under a luminous sky, their stones glowing with soft jewel-light, deep still ravines between them, immense and serene, awe-filled and peaceful, no figures.

[A] And the seventh mountain stood in the midst of these, and it excelled them in height, resembling the seat of a throne. And fragrant trees encircled the throne. And amongst them was a tree such as I had never yet smelt, neither was any amongst them, nor were others like it.
VISUAL: A great central throne-shaped mountain rising among the others, encircled by fragrant trees of soft luminous green, one tree among them brighter and more beautiful than all the rest, awe-filled and reverent, no figures.

[A] It had a fragrance beyond all fragrance, and its leaves and blooms and wood never wither. And its fruit is beautiful, and resembles the dates of a palm. Then I said: how beautiful is this tree, and fragrant, and its leaves are fair, and its blooms very delightful in appearance.
VISUAL: A single magnificent tree of soft glowing green and gold standing upon the throne-mountain, its blossoms luminous, its fruit beautiful, a gentle radiance about it, awe-filled and serene, no figures, only the tree.

[A] Then the holy angel Michael, who was with me, and who was their leader, answered, and said: this high mountain which you have seen, whose summit is like the throne of God, is his throne, where the Holy and Great One, the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, will sit, when he shall come down to visit the earth with goodness.
VISUAL: The great throne-mountain crowned with a soft descending light from a luminous height, the fragrant trees encircling it below, immense and reverent, a place kept for a holy visitation, no figures.

[A] And as for this fragrant tree, said the angel, no mortal is permitted to touch it, until the great judgement, when he shall take vengeance on all, and bring everything to its consummation for ever. Then it shall be given to the righteous and the holy.
VISUAL: The luminous tree upon the throne-mountain, set apart and waiting, a soft light held about it, a sense of something kept and promised, reverent and serene, no figures, only the tree and the keeping light.

[A] Its fruit shall be for food to the elect. It shall be transplanted to the holy place, to the temple of the Lord, the Eternal King. Then shall they rejoice with joy, and be glad, and into the holy place shall they enter. And the fragrance thereof shall be in their bones, and they shall live a long life on earth, such as your fathers lived.
VISUAL: A vision of the luminous tree transplanted beside a great temple of soft light, faint joyful presences entering toward it, a gentle radiance over all of it, deeply peaceful and reverent, no resolved figures.

[A] And in their days shall no sorrow, nor plague, nor torment, nor calamity touch them. So the journey that began in the prison of fire ends at the tree of life, kept at the throne of God for the righteous, against the day when sorrow itself shall end. The book of the watchers closes not in darkness, but in a fragrance kept for the faithful.
VISUAL: The luminous tree upon the throne-mountain under a soft and gentle light, the fragrant grove around it, a deep peace settled over the whole scene, serene and reverent, no figures, only the tree and the quiet light.

## PART FOURTEEN — The Garden of Righteousness

[A] And from there the angel carried me toward the east, to the middle of the earth, and I beheld a blessed place, in which there were trees with sweet branches, blossoming and growing. And there I saw a holy mountain, and beneath it a stream of water flowed forth from the east, and its course was toward the south.
VISUAL: A gentle and blessed land at the middle of the earth, fragrant trees in soft bloom, a clear stream flowing from beneath a holy mountain, warm and peaceful, painterly and serene, no figures.

[A] And I saw toward the east another mountain, higher than this, and between them a deep and narrow ravine. And in it ran a stream beneath the mountain. And toward the west there was another mountain, lower, and a deep and dry ravine between them, and another deep and dry ravine at the ends of the three mountains.
VISUAL: A range of holy mountains under a luminous sky, deep still ravines between them, a clear stream running in the gentle dark beneath, immense and serene, awe-filled and peaceful, no figures.

[A] And all the ravines were deep and narrow, formed of hard rock, and trees were not planted upon them. And I marvelled at the rocks, and I marvelled at the ravine. Yes, I marvelled greatly. So the scribe was shown the shape of the holy places, the mountains and the waters kept at the heart of the world.
VISUAL: A vast and reverent landscape of holy mountains and deep clear waters under a soft luminous sky, immense and still, a place set apart at the centre of the earth, awe-filled and peaceful, no figures.

[A] And then I said: for what object is this blessed land, which is entirely filled with trees, and this accursed valley between? Then Raphael, the holy angel who was with me, answered, and said: this accursed valley is for those who are accursed for ever. Here shall all the accursed be gathered together.
VISUAL: A deep and shadowed valley set between bright and blessed mountains, a hard darkness gathered in its depths against the surrounding light, grave and still, no figures, only the parted land.

[A] In the last days there shall be upon them the spectacle of righteous judgement, in the presence of the righteous for ever. Here shall the merciful bless the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King. And the blessed shall look upon the accursed, and shall understand. So even the valley of judgement serves the mercy of God, that the righteous may see, and bless, and understand.
VISUAL: The blessed mountains under a high warm light, the shadowed valley below them held in stillness, a sense of judgement and mercy held together in one vast landscape, grave and reverent, no figures.

## PART FIFTEEN — Closing Reflection

[A] So ends the first of these old words, the whole of the book of the watchers, from the blessing to the tree of life. The descent upon the mountain. The teaching of forbidden things. The cry of the earth, and the sentence of heaven. The throne of fire, and the verdict spoken. The long journey to the ends of the earth, to the prison of the stars, the mountain of the dead, and the fragrant tree kept for the faithful.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning lower now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] It is a story of how good things go wrong. Not through ignorance, but through wanting — wanting what was not given, taking what was not offered, crossing a line that was set for love and not for cruelty. And yet, even here, the last word was not the fall. It was the tree of life, and the promise of an earth made clean, and a generation far off that would be blessed.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath the deepening dark, the first stars steady above it, a quiet promise held in the night, serene, no figures.

[A] For now, let the words rest where they have fallen. Let the night be still. You do not need to carry the story. You do not need to carry anything at all. The earth keeps its order. The stars keep their courses, save those that chose to wander, and even they are held. And the patient light, the light that was promised first and kept for last, keeps watch over the faithful while they rest.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. And be at peace, until we open the old book again, and the scribe is carried higher still, to the secrets of the heavens, and the parables of the one who shall come. Until then — rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
