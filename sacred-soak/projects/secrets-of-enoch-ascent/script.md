[A] And the men who had led me, said the scribe, left me at the edge of the seventh heaven, and turned, and went back, and I did not see them again. And I remained alone, at the edge of the great light, and I was afraid, and I fell upon my face.

## PART TWENTY-THREE — Closing Reflection

[A] So we have gone up all seven heavens tonight with the old scribe — the storehouses of snow and dew, the dim place where the old ones wait, the garden of light with its tree of life, the heaven of the sun and its singing creatures, the watchers who sang again, the bright governors of the sixth, and at the last the great light, and the throne, and the garments of glory.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the storehouses, or the gates of the sun, or the ranks of the bright ones. It was all set down long ago by a scribe who walked with God, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.

[A] So let the heavens rest where they have opened, and let the night be still. What is small is not turned away, but lifted, and kept, and made to shine. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. The ascent is finished, and the scribe stands shining before the throne — and another night we will hear what the Lord told him there, the secrets of how all things were made.
