VISUAL: A dim warm dwelling at dawn, a soft gentle light within, a deep peace, deeply restful, painterly and serene, no figures.

## PART TWENTY - Closing Reflection

[A] So tonight we have kept company with the gentle farewell of Abraham - the kind messenger, the welcome at the oak, the washing and the meal, the tender news, the love of the world, the seeing of the earth, the compassion, the mercy of the judgment, the gentle coming of rest, and the carrying-home by the angels.
VISUAL: The single oil lamp again, burning low now in the dark stone chamber, the scroll closed beside it, the amber light soft and fading, dust settling slowly in the still air, intimate and quiet, the reading done.

[A] Hold none of it too tightly. You do not need to carry the farewell, or the vision, or the mercy. It was set down long ago, that it might be a comfort to a generation far off. Perhaps tonight, that generation is you.
VISUAL: A wide calm horizon at the very end of dusk, a thin warm line of gold beneath a deepening blue, the first stars appearing above it, calm and vast, the day letting go, no figures, only the turning sky.

[A] So let the old story rest where it has fallen, and let the night be still. Those the Lord loves, he calls home gently, with kindness, and honour; the judgment is tempered with a great mercy; and the end comes not as a terror, but as a soft rest, a being-carried-home, like sleep at the close of a long good day. There is nothing to fear in the gentle farewell. Rest in that, while the night turns slowly over you.
VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You are dealt with gently, by a Lord who is kind to his friends, tempers judgment with mercy, and gathers those he loves home softly, like sleep at the end of a good day. Another night we will draw again from the deep quarry of the old books. But for now, be at peace, and rest. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
