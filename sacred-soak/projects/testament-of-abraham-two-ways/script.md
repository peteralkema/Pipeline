VISUAL: The vast still night sky once more, the faint band of stars turning slowly overhead, deeper now and calmer, the dark soft and complete, endless and peaceful, the long quiet of the heavens.

[A] So breathe out. Let the day go from you. You are weighed by a merciful hand that longs to save you, prayed for by loving hearts, received whenever you turn, however late. Do not judge, but pray; do not fear, but rest. Another night we will lie down with Jacob, and see the ladder joining earth and heaven. But for now, rest in the mercy wider than you fear. Until then - rest.
VISUAL: A single faint star high in a deep and gentle dark, holding steady, the night calm and boundless around it, utterly still, the last soft light, nothing else.
