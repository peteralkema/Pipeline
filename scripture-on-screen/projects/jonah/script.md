## ACT 5 / CLOSE — THE ANGRY PROPHET AND THE PLANT

[A] When Nineveh was spared, Jonah was furious. Not relieved. Not glad. Furious. And in his anger, he finally confessed the real reason he had run in the first place — and it is one of the most revealing prayers in scripture.

[A] Lord, is this not what I said would happen when I was still at home? he prayed. Is this not why I fled to Tarshish? Jonah had run, not because he feared God would judge Nineveh — but because he feared God would forgive it.

[A] There it is — the real heart of the story. Jonah did not want his enemies to receive mercy. He would rather they were destroyed.
