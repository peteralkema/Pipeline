## 1. WHY THIS CHANNEL, AND WHY THE PIVOT

### 1.1 What the data actually said

The pivot was not a hunch. Four independent signals converged, pulled live from NexLev and the channel's own analytics (09 July 2026):

1. **The channel's own best organic video is about ethics** — "Ethical Decision Making Frameworks" outperformed the rest of a 1,225-video library.
2. **The audience is 35–44, evenly split male/female.** Credential-seeking working professionals, not the eighteen-year-old self-help market the old positioning chased.
3. **The traffic engine is search, overwhelmingly.** ~74% from YouTube search + external embeds (1,141 EXT_URL + 1,114 YT_SEARCH over 90 days). Suggested delivered 81 views. Browse ≈ 0. Subscribers drove 168 views from a base of 6,090.
4. **The outlier scan found the lane already printing.** Ethics Unwrapped — a 40K-sub channel — pulls 700–820K views per video on an animated concept glossary (Deontology 822K, Virtue Ethics 732K, Utilitarianism 692K), monetised. Ethics case studies (Challenger, 396K) and "explained in two minutes" formats print alongside them. A 3,340-sub channel hit 207K on "Morals vs Ethics Explained."

A discrete "AI ethics" scan came back polluted junk. **Therefore: ethics is the FRAME. AI is the sharpest spearhead TOPIC inside it.** Never invert this.

### 1.2 What the channel actually is

**A search-utility library with a dead feed and a dead subscriber base.** Say this plainly, because every strategic error on this channel has come from pretending it is a feed channel.

- Subscribers (6,090) already clear the monetisation threshold. They are **a number, not an audience.**
- The wall is **watch-hours only.** ~6,172 watch-minutes / 90 days annualises to ~410 hours against the 4,000 needed. **~10%.**
- Two-minute-AVD micro-lessons will never close that gap. The math is fatal *in the old format* and entirely tractable the moment format changes.

### 1.3 Why not start a fresh channel

Because the 1,225 legacy videos are **search-authority substrate that already ranks.** New ethics content compounds on a domain YouTube already trusts. A new channel starts at zero. The argument for keeping Success Coach is **not** the 6K dead subs — it is the ranking authority. Do not delete the old library.

### 1.4 The monetisation math

- Subs: **done.**
- Watch-hours needed: **~240,000 minutes/year.**
- A library of ~50 evergreen videos at ~6.5 min runtime, holding even a 5-minute AVD, at even 500 views/day aggregate → comfortably past threshold.
- Because it is **evergreen search**, it accrues and keeps accruing rather than spiking and dying.

**This is a credible path back to YPP within months of consistent publishing. Consistency is the only hard requirement — and consistency is exactly what the flywheel makes possible where a solo human could not.**

## 3. VISUAL IDENTITY — LOCKED

### 3.1 The palette

Chosen on evidence, not taste. Visual design accounts for ~46% of credibility judgments (Stanford Web Credibility); colour drives up to 90% of first impression. For a channel whose entire promise is *trust my judgment on right and wrong*, packaging does credibility work before a word is spoken.

Blue is the trust colour — and when ~95% of a category uses blue, plain blue *blends in*. Deep teal blends blue's trustworthiness with green's learning/growth signal, and is more distinctive than navy. Operator chose the bluer end of that range.

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Petrol (PRIMARY)** | `#0d5a72` | Backgrounds, brand mark, key headline word |
| Petrol Deep | `#08394a` | Gradients, scrims, depth |
| **Amber (ACCENT)** | `#f4b73f` | One highlight word; the mark's focal dot. **Sparingly.** |
| Paper | `#f7f9fa` | Bright base, white headlines, clean space |
| Ink | `#0f2027` | Dark text on light, contrast anchor |

**Three colours plus neutrals. Consistency is itself a trust signal. Do not add a fourth.**

### 3.2 The mark

**A balance scale, with an amber focal dot at the pivot.** Recurring, on every thumbnail, and as the closing visual of every video. Generic public-domain symbolism — no IP risk — and instantly legible as "ethics / judgment."

### 3.3 Thumbnails — the alternating A/B system

**Two treatments, alternated across uploads:**

- **A — Petrol field.** Dark petrol background, white mark, one amber hook-word.
- **B — Bright paper.** Bright pale background, petrol mark, one amber hook-word.

**Why alternate:** thumbnails appear *next to each other* — channel page, suggested, search. An all-dark or all-light channel turns into visual mush. Alternating high-contrast dark/light means whichever the surrounding thumbnails are, yours pops. This turns a lucky contrast effect into a permanent structural feature. It also directly kills the feed-monotony failure the old dark thumbnails produced.

**Bottom-left text anchor.** Not adaptive, not detected — a fixed rule. It reads as a documentary lower-third chyron, which reinforces the authoritative register, and it structurally clears faces/subjects that sit high in frame. Do not confuse this with the vision-judged anchor engine (canonical principle #20) — that is deliberately NOT used here. If this channel ever becomes face-forward, revisit.

**Thumb schema:** `{subject, title, subtitle}` — three keys, exactly.
- `subject`: bright flat-vector conceptual illustration, petrol/amber, generous empty space upper-left for the headline, **no text in the image**.
- `title`: bold condensed, one amber hook-word.
- `subtitle`: lowercase, and it must **open a curiosity gap** — never restate the title. "would you pull the lever?" not "a famous dilemma."

**Never shock-clickbait.** It would undercut the entire trust play, which is the channel's only real asset.

### 3.4 The register (`style_suffix`)

Current, live in `success-coach/channel.json`:

> `bright high-key editorial photography, clean modern 2026 setting, crisp even natural daylight, deep focus and sharp clarity, vivid but tasteful saturated color, cool blue-forward grade with a warm accent, contemporary professional and credible, optimistic documentary realism`

**What it replaced, and why it was killing the channel:**

> ~~`cinematic photoreal modern documentary look... soft natural directional light, shallow depth of field, gentle film grain, slightly desaturated muted palette, 35mm cinematic feel, calm and observational`~~

That old suffix says "modern documentary" and *means* Final Hours. Read the operative words: **muted, grain, shallow, desaturated, calm-observational.** Every one pulls the model toward dim and moody. It produced videos the operator described as *"a collection of stills from Batman with a voice over"* — tasteful, quiet, and boring, which for an ethics channel is worse than dark.

## 4. FORMAT — FACELESS, NARRATOR-LED, CONTENT-FIRST

### 4.1 The decision, and the evidence behind it

**No host. No avatar. No presenter. No "Coach Alex."**

Five format options were built and reviewed (illustrated host, stylized-cinematic figure, iconic mark only, editorial scenes only, sophisticated mascot). None of the avatars pulled. That was a **signal**, and the research agrees with it:

- A **photorealistic AI host that tries to pass as human is disqualifying** for a trust-dependent niche. Audiences read AI influencers as "authentically fake"; a fake authority figure is the one thing that can sink an authority channel. 2026 guidance is explicit: avoid niches where audience trust requires a verified human face. **Ethics is the definition of that niche.**
- A **clearly-synthetic, non-deceptive presenter** is fine where the host explains rather than embodies trust. **But the market has already voted:** School of Life, Einzelgänger, Pursuit of Wonder, Academy of Ideas, Ethics Unwrapped — the proven giants of this exact lane are all faceless-narrator + designed visuals.
- **The voice is the trust anchor, not a face.**

**Therefore: the recurring "interaction anchor" viewers need is a consistent VISUAL IDENTITY** — same mark, same palette, same title system, same sign-off, every single time. That is the parasocial hook for a faceless brand.

### 4.2 The narrator

**Inworld `Duncan`, `speaking_rate: 0.85`.** (Capital D. Inworld model is fixed at `inworld-tts-2`; there is no mode/quality key — "balanced" is just default behaviour. Do not invent a key.)

0.85 is a deliberate slowing below natural pace. Slower reads as *considered*, weighty, authoritative; it gives moral statements room to land. **The script must be written FOR that pace** — short weighted sentences, frequent full stops, turns given their own beat. Long run-on sentences drag at 0.85.

Wiring is live: `patch_inworld_speaking_rate.py` maps `channel.json → speaking_rate` to Inworld's `audioConfig.speakingRate` (`recreation_pipeline.py` ~line 918). Absent key = 1.0.

### 4.3 AI disclosure

**Altered Content = Yes, on every upload.** Non-negotiable, and not merely compliance: 2026 data shows audiences score brands *higher* on trust when AI use is labelled honestly than when it is hidden. **Honesty about the synthesis is itself a trust asset for an ethics channel.** Our human-authored scripts and original analysis are exactly the "meaningful commentary / transformation" the July-2025 policy requires for monetisation.

## 5. THE ANTI-PATTERN BAN — THE MOST IMPORTANT SECTION IN THIS DOCUMENT

### 5.1 Why this exists

Every channel in the portfolio has drifted toward the same dim, painterly, candlelit register. Diagnosed on 08–09 July, and again on the first Lazarus render before that. **Three compounding causes:**

1. **Model prior / mode collapse.** Asked for "cinematic," "evocative," "emotional," a generative model returns the *median* of everything ever captioned that way: candlelight, chiaroscuro, painterly, muted, slow, "timeless." Every channel collapses to the same dim oil-painting mean **unless actively pushed off it.**

2. **Darkness hides model weakness.** Shadow, silhouette, empty rooms, and shallow focus conceal what image models render badly — hands, faces mid-expression, crowds, text, bright even light that exposes every flaw. **"Moody and dark" is not an aesthetic choice. It is the model steering toward what it can get away with, dressed up as tasteful restraint.**

3. **Undocumented channels inherit the loudest doctrine.** Final Hours is the most richly documented channel in the canon. Absent an equally strong counter-register, every authoring pass pattern-matches to it. Success Coach had a voice and a thumbnail block and **no visual storytelling doctrine at all** — so it inherited Final Hours by default.

**Positive instructions alone ("be bright and modern") get swallowed by the prior. Only enumerated bans bite.**

### 5.2 BANNED — every visual line, no exceptions

- candles, candlelight, lanterns, firelight as primary light
- painterly, oil-painting, chiaroscuro, Rembrandt lighting
- Victorian, antique, sepia, "timeless," "aged," parchment
- darkness-as-default; empty unlit rooms; heavy shadow as the emotional carrier
- silhouettes and backs-of-heads as a crutch
- symbolic-object-in-the-dark substituting for a human situation
- dust motes in light shafts; single candle on a desk; rain-on-window melancholy
- slow, static, museum-quiet "mood" framing
- **and the sneakier ones that hid inside the old `style_suffix`:** muted, desaturated, gentle film grain, shallow depth of field, "calm and observational," dusk

### 5.3 REQUIRED — every visual line

- bright, high-key, crisp even natural daylight
- clean modern contemporary settings (offices, hospitals, courtrooms, classrooms, streets, labs)
- **real people in real situations**, dignified and naturalistic
- deep focus, sharp clarity, vivid but tasteful colour
- emotion carried by pose, situation, and interaction — **not** by lighting, and not by theatrical faces
- the frame should read as *observational documentary of modern life*, never as a noir mood piece

### 5.4 Enforcement

Doctrine that is not enforced is a wish. **Build a VISUAL-safety keyword pass** (backlog, §8) that flags banned terms in any `VISUAL:` line at the zero-spend gate, exactly as over-length beats are flagged today. Until it exists, the authoring gate script greps for: `candle|dusk|dim|darkness|painterly|sepia|victorian|gloom|moody|muted|grain|shadowy`.

**Batch 01 (20 scripts) passed this scan with zero violations. That is the baseline. Never regress.**

### 5.5 The cross-channel principle (graduate to canonical)

> **A channel with no explicit visual doctrine defaults to the model's prior, which is the dim painterly mean — and Final Hours is simply that mean written down. Every channel needs enumerated ANTI-PATTERNS, not merely a positive register. Any channel that genuinely wants an item from the ban list must consciously CLAIM it in its own doctrine, never inherit it.**

## 6. SCRIPT DOCTRINE

### 6.1 Hard format invariants (parser-enforced)

- Bare key-value header: `channel:` `title:` `description:` `tags:` — `channel: success-coach`
- Section markers exactly `## COLD OPEN` and `## PART ONE`…`## PART SIX` (parser exits 2 on anything else)
- `[A]` beat lines, each followed by exactly one `VISUAL:` line — **1:1 pairing, always**
- **No beat over 55 words**
- **No digits in narration.** Spell out: "nine in every ten," "half a century," "the next hundred years"
- No `[sigh]`. Max two `[pause]`, attached to real spoken words, never standalone
- Slug rule: `^[a-z0-9][a-z0-9-]{0,60}$`

### 6.2 The six-part spine

Uniform across all three content types. This is what makes the batch a clean experiment.

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| **COLD OPEN** | Put the viewer *inside* the dilemma. Never open with a definition. |
| **ONE** | Build the idea. Why it is hard / what happened. |
| **TWO** | The first answer, or the power of the idea. |
| **THREE** | The counter-answer, or where it strains. |
| **FOUR** | The turn — the variation, correction, or objection that makes it personal. |
| **FIVE** | **THE AI TURN.** Always. The machine at the lever. |
| **SIX** | The weight you carry out. Earned, unresolved, honest. |

### 6.3 Length

**~800–860 narration words ≈ 6.3–6.8 min at Duncan 0.85** (~127 wpm effective).

Length is a **frozen variable** across a batch. When comparing topics, a longer video wins on watch-time for reasons that have nothing to do with topic. *(Known debt: `the-trolley-problem` is 1,018 words / 8.0 min against a batch clustered at 747–861. Trim to ~850 before staging, or accept the confound knowingly.)*

Test longer case-studies (12–20 min) as a **deliberate, isolated experiment** in a later batch. Never let it drift in by accident.

### 6.4 Voice on the page

Measured. A little grave. Trusted-documentary. Short weighted sentences. The narrator has thought about hard questions for a long time and is not hurrying.

- **Take every position seriously before criticising it.** The channel's credibility rests on steelmanning.
- **Correct the popular story where it is wrong** (the Pinto memo was not about the Pinto; Milgram's archives are messier than the legend). Honesty over a good headline — that *is* the product.
- **Never resolve cheaply.** Part Six earns its weight by refusing a tidy answer.
- **The sign-off, verbatim, every video:** *"I am glad you thought it through with me. There is always more to weigh. And we will weigh it together, here."*

### 6.5 `[CARD:]` markers

Placed at conceptual hinge points, alongside the `VISUAL:` line. They mark where a future **concept card** graphic will slot in (§7). They cost nothing now and make that upgrade drop-in rather than a re-authoring job.

## 7. THE CONCEPT-CARD LAYER (backlog, channel-defining)

**Ethics is abstract, and abstraction is exactly where rendered scenes fail.** A photoreal hospital corridor shows a *place*; it cannot show a *relationship* — "five lives against one," "same maths, opposite feeling," "consequentialism vs deontology." A designed graphic makes structure legible in a way no rendered picture can. That is **sense-making**, not decoration.

The boundary the operator drew correctly: **not corporate slides and bullets.** A bullet list *replaces* the narration (redundant — the thumbnail-caption problem). A conceptual graphic *does what the words cannot*: it externalises the structure so the viewer's brain can hold it. This carries cognitive load the voice cannot, and it is a large part of why School of Life, Vox, and Kurzgesagt hold attention.

**Build `make_concept_card.py`** — deterministic petrol/amber SVG cards (the scale, a 5-vs-1 split, a mapping arrow, a boundary line), rendered as a beat's still in place of a fal image. Reuses the thumbnail text machinery and the locked identity.

**Do NOT prompt fal for diagrams.** Image models garble text, precise relationships, and clean charts. This must be deterministic vector, exactly like the thumbnails.

**Sequencing: build it AFTER batch 01 reads.** Prove the base format earns traction first, then add concept cards as the retention-deepening layer — the same additive discipline as Kling.

## 8. OPERATING RULES & BACKLOG

### 8.1 Render discipline

- **Ken Burns floor, Kling additively.** Batch 01 runs `--kling-count 0`. Animation spend only after CTR+AVD justify it. (Cost is driven by Kling count, not beat count. Ken Burns is free.)
- **Gate at the stills-review seam** for anything new. The first bright-register render was checked at stills before any animation spend — that is how the scrub was proven for $0.
- **`batch_inbox/` is gitignored.** Scripts travel to the box by `rsync -avP -e "ssh -p 443"`, never by git. Config (`channel.json`) travels by git.
- Edit on **LAPTOP** (`~/Projects/Pipeline`, `python3`), push, **BOX** (`~/Pipeline`, `python`) pulls. Never hand-edit on the box.

### 8.2 Reading the data

- **First-48-hour CTR + AVD drive distribution.** Impressions are an output, not a lever.
- **Promote on a vein, not a spike.** Two independent signals must agree before doctrine moves.
- **Read the median, never the mean.**
- Batch 01 is deliberately **exploratory**: 20 topics, four categories, everything else frozen. When the outliers surface, exploit the *pattern* — near-clones, then role/institution transplants, then variable-isolation experiments.
- The big fork batch 01 will answer: **does this channel win on evergreen concepts, narrative case studies, or the AI spearhead?** That answer shapes batch 02.

### 8.3 Backlog (ordered)

1. **Trim `the-trolley-problem` to ~850 words** before staging — restore the frozen-length condition.
2. **Stage + render batch 01** (`stage_batch.py`, `--kling-count 0`), staggered publish, both/all categories interleaved so no category gets a calendar advantage.
3. **VISUAL-safety keyword gate** (§5.4) — make the ban list bite at the zero-spend gate.
4. **Thumbnail identity as a pipeline RULE** — auto-alternate treatment A/B by index; the scale as a reusable overlay asset. Currently hand-managed; it will drift.

### 8.4 Durable lessons banked here

- **OAuth:** an app left in *Testing* mode kills its refresh token after 7 days (`invalid_grant`). **Set the consent screen to Production BEFORE minting.** Success Coach uses its own `success-coach/auth.py` + `upload.py`, not the shared `upload_episode.py`.
- **Thumbnail curiosity gap:** a matched text+image pair means the caption is already consumed and there is no reason to click. The gap must force a question only the video answers.
- **Never trust Finder's "Date Modified" on git-managed files.** Git sets mtime at checkout, not authorship. Ask `git log`, never the filesystem.
- **The presenter question is a trap.** This niche is not won by a face or a mascot. It is won by being the clearest, most valuable, best-packaged explanation of a genuinely interesting question. The operator arrived at this before the research did.
