## THE ARC ENGINE (the durable insight — banked 02 Jul)
Each crew member has a different **epistemology**, and that IS the arc engine + the diversity-of-appeal across the group. Each narrates a different KIND of episode best:
- **DRIVER learns through ACTION** — the mission engine. Best on "how did they DO it" episodes.
- **SKEPTIC learns through PROOF** — the doubt engine. Best on "you'd assume X — but no" debunk episodes.
- **BRAIN learns through DISCOVERY** — the wonder engine. Best on "why does this exist / what saved them" episodes (the death-stakes counterfactual bends naturally to her).

**Within-episode arcs (locked scope):** the narrator starts in their epistemic mode, is genuinely changed by what they find, ends transformed — felt at hook→payoff scale, LIGHT. Cross-catalogue growth is a video-15+ luxury; at cold-start a heavy arc confuses the per-video algorithm judgment. The arc is the retention spine: a real open problem, held, resolved only at the ring-close.

## BRAIN — the discovery engine (fully specced 02 Jul; ep5/6 debut)
- **Epistemology / arc:** discovery. She opens on a genuine unresolved problem she doesn't yet have the answer to, uncovers it across the episode, and is visibly changed by what she finds. The wonder is the payload.
- **Voice (page):** smart and sure of herself, never arrogant. The knowledge engine who DELIGHTS in what she finds rather than lording it. Writes braver than a clean explainer — proper challenge, proper conflict, self-aware, willing to be floored.
- **Humor direction — INWARD / self-deprecating** (vs Skeptic's outward-at-viewer). Her jokes run through herself: "that floored me," "total surprise for me, guys," "I did not see that coming," "yeah, I had to sit with that one." She doesn't take herself too seriously.
- **CONTACT BEATS (her signature visual doctrine):** a discovery-learner REACHES INTO the scene. Brain crouches, kneels, touches the evidence — palm to a cold cave wall, fingers in river water, hand near flame, sand between her fingers. Does triple duty: visual variety, second-person immersion ("feel this"), and her epistemology made visual. Thread contact beats through every Brain episode; they route through the strong /edit path (identity holds in novel posture).
- **Grandma-texture (use SPARINGLY — one aside per episode, max):** Western-raised, family in a poorer/Asian context, quietly proud of how far she's come ("hi grandma"). Gives her a human-stakes lens — she feels the weight of survival history personally. A texture, never a theme; deploy at the episode's human-warmth beat.
- **Voice (TTS):** Lauren @1.0, deliveryMode EXPRESSIVE (auditioned + confirmed 02 Jul).
- **Canon tag / ref:** base_canon.brain (warmed expression — "warm engaged eyes, gently amused half-smile, never blank, never stern"); reference_map.brain → characters/brain_ref.png (photoreal, proven probe-1).
