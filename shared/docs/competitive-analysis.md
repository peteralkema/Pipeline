That last fact is worth sitting with. The empty lane is the strategic prize.

## PART 4 — Cross-cutting strategic insights

Six themes that emerged across all 30 channels and matter to every Peter decision:

**One — the breakthrough timing is consistent.** Across these 30 channels, breakthrough happens between days 30 and 250 for fast-positive cases, days 250-1000 for medium cases, and days 1000-1600 for long-haul cases. Final Hours at day 1 is firmly inside the normal distribution. The flat period is the cost of admission, not a signal of failure.

**Two — production cadence drives the algorithmic test.** The fastest breakouts (Arthur, Matt, MM Loadout, Dr Bob, Backyard Bankroll, Brand Report) all uploaded weekly or more frequently during their breakthrough window. The Majestic Studios breakout corresponded to a 16-video posting blitz in one month. Cadence matters more than perfection during the test phase.

**Three — title formulas concentrate around a few structural patterns.** "Specific Person + Specific Action" (NG, Matt, Arthur), "Specific Number + Specific Category" (Backyard Bankroll, Broken Canada, Brand Report), "Place + Year" (Matt, Arthur), "Hidden Truth + Specific Entity" (Rise and Fall America, Mr. Death). Final Hours' more-poetic title style ("He Could Have Run. Instead, He Picked Up His Violin.") is distinctive — possibly an asset, possibly a conversion penalty. Worth testing variants.

**Four — RPM matters more than view count for revenue.** Broken Canada ($6.43 RPM) and Findicate ($4-5 RPM territory) earn more per view than history channels ($2.50-3.50 RPM). The RPM advantage of professional/business/finance content is real. Success Coach's positioning in AI-era career transformation puts it in the higher-RPM band, which is why it earns more per video than Final Hours will at equivalent scale. Worth accepting that Success Coach's slower view growth is offset by higher per-view revenue.

**Five — outlier scores are early indicators, not late ones.** Channels with 5x+ outlier scores in their first 60-100 days went on to break out by month 6-9 with high reliability. Final Hours' current outlier score (whatever it is at video 3) will tell you more about month 9 than any individual video's view count will. Watch the outlier score climb across the first 10 videos as the early signal.

**Six — the lane Peter chose for Channel 3 is genuinely empty.** Not "mostly empty" — empty. This is the strongest strategic finding across all three channels. Final Hours and Success Coach are entering crowded lanes with strong adjacent operators (which provides calibration evidence and execution playbooks). Channel 3 is entering an empty lane, which means you get to define the category. Both are valuable; the latter is rarer.
