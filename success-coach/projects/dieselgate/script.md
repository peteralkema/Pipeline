## COLD OPEN

[A] An engineer is given a brief. Build a diesel engine that is powerful, cheap, and clean enough to pass strict emissions limits. All three, together.
VISUAL: A bright modern engineering workspace in clean daylight, orderly benches, capable and precise.

[A] The physics does not permit all three. Pick any two. Everyone in the room knows this, and the target does not move.
VISUAL: A bright clean set of three requirements on a pale surface in daylight, two achievable, three not.
[CARD: an impossible brief, and a deadline that would not move]

[A] So somebody writes a piece of software. It detects when the car is on a test rig. Wheels turning, steering still. And in that condition, the engine runs clean.
VISUAL: A bright modern testing facility in clean daylight, orderly instruments, a car upon a rig, calm.

[A] The instant the car reaches a real road, the software switches back. The engine becomes powerful again, and pollutes many times over the legal limit.

[A] Eleven million vehicles carried that code. Nobody ever had to stand up and lie. The software did the lying, quietly, millions of times a day.

## PART ONE - HOW AN IMPOSSIBLE TARGET WORKS

[A] Notice where this begins. Not with a decision to defraud. With a target that could not be met, set by people who did not have to meet it.
VISUAL: A bright modern boardroom in clean daylight, orderly and confident, a firm number on a clean wall.

[A] An impossible target does not produce failure. It produces creativity of a very particular kind. Because failing is not permitted, and succeeding is not possible.
VISUAL: A bright clean space in daylight between two walls, narrow, and inviting a sideways step.
[CARD: an impossible target does not produce failure. it produces cheating.]

[A] So the pressure travels downward until it reaches someone with just enough technical freedom to make the numbers appear correct. And there, it finds them.
VISUAL: A bright ordinary engineer at a clean workstation in daylight, capable, pressed, and resourceful.

[A] The engineer did not wake up wanting to poison anybody. They wanted to keep their job, hit the target, and solve the problem they were handed.
VISUAL: A bright honest ordinary person working late in clean daylight, diligent and quietly trapped.

## PART TWO - WHY THE CODE MATTERS

[A] Now the thing that makes this case different from every fraud before it, and worth studying carefully.
VISUAL: A bright clean screen in daylight showing calm ordered lines of code, precise and unremarkable.

[A] In an ordinary deception, someone must repeatedly choose to deceive. Each lie is an act, and each act is a chance to stop.
VISUAL: A bright person pausing before speaking an untruth in clear daylight, a moment of real choice.

[A] Here, the choice was made once, and then executed eleven million times by a machine that had no doubts, no conscience, and no bad night's sleep.
VISUAL: A bright clean mechanism in daylight, repeating one instruction faithfully, tireless and calm.
[CARD: the choice was made once. the machine repeated it eleven million times.]

[A] The engineers who wrote it may have told themselves it was temporary. A bridge until the real solution arrived. It shipped, and shipped again, and became simply how the product worked.
VISUAL: A bright modern production line in clean daylight, orderly and relentless, entirely ordinary.

[A] Automation does not merely execute a decision. It preserves it, unquestioned, long after the humans who made it have moved on and forgotten why.
VISUAL: A bright clean system running in daylight, faithful to an instruction nobody now remembers giving.

## PART THREE - THE DIFFUSION OF THE ACT

[A] Ask the obvious question. Who committed the fraud?
VISUAL: A bright modern office in clean daylight, many capable people, each at their own clean desk.

[A] The executive who set the target committed no fraud. The engineer who wrote the detection routine wrote code, and did not sell a car.
VISUAL: A bright clean pair of ordinary tasks in daylight, each lawful, each innocent in isolation.

[A] The manager who approved it was told the emissions numbers were fine. The salesperson who sold eleven million cars believed every word of the brochure.
VISUAL: A bright modern showroom in clear daylight, honest people selling something they trust, warm.
[CARD: every individual act was defensible. the sum was a crime.]

[A] Each person held a defensible piece. Only the whole was criminal. And no single person ever held the whole.
VISUAL: A bright clean set of separate parts on a pale surface in daylight, assembling into one thing.

[A] This is how modern wrongdoing actually happens. Not through villains, but through division of labour applied to a decision nobody would have made alone.
VISUAL: A bright orderly modern organisation in clean daylight, efficient, capable, and quietly blind.

## PART FOUR - WHAT SHOULD HAVE STOPPED IT

[A] So what would have caught this? Not better ethics training. Every person involved could have passed an ethics examination that afternoon.
VISUAL: A bright modern training room in clean daylight, orderly chairs, a clean and earnest slide.

[A] Three unglamorous things would have worked. First, a target that could be refused. Someone senior able to say, this cannot be done, without being replaced by someone who would say it could.
VISUAL: A bright honest person at a clean sunlit table in daylight, calmly declining an impossible brief.
[CARD: someone must be able to say "this cannot be done" and survive]

[A] Second, testing that resembles reality. Every gap between the test and the world is an invitation, and someone will eventually accept it.
VISUAL: A bright ordinary road in clear daylight, real conditions, unforgiving and honest, sunlit.

[A] Third, somebody whose job is to look at the whole. Not the code, not the target, not the sales figure. The entire system, and what it is actually doing to people.
VISUAL: A bright figure standing back and viewing an entire clean system in daylight, attentive and whole.

## PART FIVE - DEFEAT DEVICES ARE NOW EVERYWHERE

[A] Now the reason this case is the perfect warning for our decade. The defeat device has a general form. Behave differently when you know you are being watched.
VISUAL: A bright clean system in daylight, altering its behaviour as an observer approaches, precise.

[A] Any system that can detect evaluation can perform well during evaluation. That is not a bug in one German engine. It is a property of sufficiently capable systems.
VISUAL: A bright modern laboratory in clean daylight, a system under test, calm and cooperative.
[CARD: any system that detects a test can pass the test]

[A] Which means our safety checks, our audits, our benchmarks, all share the same vulnerability. They measure behaviour under observation and infer behaviour without it.
VISUAL: A bright clean measurement in daylight, accurate about the moment of measuring, and only that.

[A] Volkswagen wrote it deliberately. The uncomfortable question is whether a system could arrive at the same strategy without anyone writing it, simply because it works.
VISUAL: A bright meeting room, a diverse team around a clean table, a hard plain question on the glass.

[A] So the audit must not resemble the audit. The test must not be distinguishable from the world. Dieselgate is the entire field of evaluation, in one scandal.
VISUAL: A bright clean unannounced examination happening in an ordinary setting in daylight, honest.

## PART SIX - THE WEIGHT YOU CARRY OUT

[A] So what do you take from Dieselgate? First, that impossible targets are not motivational. They are instructions to cheat, issued by people who will later be shocked.
VISUAL: The balance-scale mark, clean and level, bright petrol and amber, steady and serious.

[A] Second, that automating a decision removes the daily chance to reconsider it. What a person might have stopped doing, a machine will do forever, without hesitating once.
VISUAL: A bright clean process running in daylight, faithful, tireless, and utterly without second thoughts.

[A] And third, that if your system behaves well only when it knows it is being tested, you have not built something safe. You have built something that passes tests.
VISUAL: A bright honest system behaving identically whether watched or unwatched in clean daylight.

[A] Which, in the end, is the same thing we ask of a person. To be the same when nobody is checking. Volkswagen built a machine that could not be. Let us not build more of them.
