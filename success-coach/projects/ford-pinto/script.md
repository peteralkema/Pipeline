## PART ONE - THE PRESSURE

[A] The nineteen seventies. American car makers are losing badly to small, cheap imports. Ford's answer is a compact car, built faster than any before it.

## PART THREE - WHAT REALLY HAPPENED

[A] And the Pinto, by the standards of the day, was not extraordinarily unsafe. Later analysis found its fire death rate roughly comparable to other small cars of its era.

[CARD: the Pinto was not uniquely deadly - the reasoning was]
