## PART ONE - WHY HE RAN IT

[A] Milgram was working in the shadow of the war crimes trials, where men who had done monstrous things kept offering the same explanation. They were following orders.
VISUAL: A bright modern courtroom in clean daylight, formal and orderly, empty and quiet, high-key.

[A] The comfortable assumption was that such people were monsters, different in kind from us. Milgram wanted to know whether that was true.
VISUAL: A bright ordinary street of ordinary decent people in clear daylight, warm and unremarkable.

[A] So he built a situation with no threat, no punishment, no coercion at all. Only a calm authority, a plausible purpose, and a series of small escalating steps.
VISUAL: A bright clean staircase of small even steps in daylight, each one modest, ascending, sunlit.
[CARD: no threat. no punishment. only calm authority and small steps.]

[A] And a majority of ordinary people, given nothing but a mild insistence to continue, continued. That is the finding. It has never comfortably gone away.
VISUAL: A bright plain figure seated at the panel in clear daylight, ordinary, decent, and continuing.

## PART THREE - WHAT IT DOES NOT PROVE

[A] Now the correction, because the popular version of this story is too tidy, and honesty matters more than a good headline.
VISUAL: A bright clean document being read carefully in daylight, attentive and precise, high-key.

[A] Later scholars examined Milgram's own archives. The prodding was often more forceful than described. Some participants suspected the shocks were fake. Results varied enormously across versions.
VISUAL: A bright modern archive of clean ordered files in daylight, open and examined, sunlit.
[CARD: the real study was messier than the famous story]

[A] And the simple conclusion, that we are all robots waiting for orders, does not survive the data. Many people refused. Many argued. Some walked out.
VISUAL: A bright figure standing up and stepping away from the panel in clear daylight, resolute and calm.

[A] What the evidence better supports is subtler and more interesting. People did not obey because they were mindless. They obeyed because they were persuaded the cause was worthy.
VISUAL: A bright thoughtful figure genuinely convinced by a reasonable-seeming purpose, clear daylight.

[A] They were not surrendering their morality. They were being recruited into someone else's. That is a far more useful, and far more frightening, lesson.

## PART FOUR - THE REAL WARNING

[A] So the danger is not a man shouting orders. We are quite good at resisting that. The danger is a calm voice, a good cause, and a small next step.
VISUAL: A bright calm reasonable figure speaking mildly in clear daylight, credible and unthreatening.

[A] It is being told that the responsibility sits elsewhere. That the process has been approved. That this is simply how things are done here.
VISUAL: A bright clean approved procedure on a pale desk in daylight, stamped and orderly, unremarkable.
[CARD: the danger is a calm voice, a good cause, a small next step]

[A] And it is never seeing the person at the other end. Every institution that causes harm at scale has a wall in it somewhere, with a voice on the other side.
