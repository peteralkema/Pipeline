## PART ONE - HOW THE QUIET LAYOFF WORKS

[A] Start with the mechanism, because it is not what you would guess. The quiet layoff rarely fires anyone. It works through the spaces between people.
VISUAL: A brick wall with bricks missing here and there, the gaps growing.

[A] Through attrition, hiring freezes, and the slow decision to not replace those who leave. Here is the cycle. Someone resigns or retires.
VISUAL: The wall still standing but visibly thinning, light passing through the holes.

[A] In the past, the company would post the role and hire a replacement. Now a manager asks a different question.
VISUAL: A job posting on a screen being closed and archived without ever being published.

[A] Can the team absorb this work with the help of the new tools. Often the answer is yes. The role is quietly retired.
VISUAL: A draft folder filling with abandoned positions in the dark.

[A] No one was fired. A job simply ceased to exist. Multiply that across a company, then across an industry.
VISUAL: A wide staircase of figures descending, seen over time so the count thins gradually.

[A] And you get a workforce that shrinks without a single dramatic event. A layoff spread so thin across time that it never makes the news.
VISUAL: The crowd at the top of that staircase dense, the bottom nearly empty.

[A] And companies vastly prefer this. A mass layoff brings headlines, lawsuits, morale collapse, and a damaged brand. A quiet layoff brings none of those.
VISUAL: A balance sheet glowing on a dark screen, one side growing heavier and brighter.

[A] It looks like efficiency. It looks like good management. The same reduction in jobs, with all the pain moved off the company's books.
VISUAL: The cost side of that balance sheet fading quietly to nothing, no alarm, no red.

## PART TWO - THE WARNING SIGNS

[A] So how do you see something designed to be invisible. There are signs, if you know where to look.
VISUAL: A weathered barometer on a wall, the needle slowly dropping toward stormy.

[A] And they appear long before anyone says the word artificial intelligence out loud. The first sign. People leave and are not replaced.
VISUAL: The room around that barometer calm and bright and unaware.

[A] Watch the headcount of your team and the ones around you. If departures stop turning into job postings, the quiet layoff has already begun.
VISUAL: A team photo on a wall with several faces faded almost to blank.

[A] The work does not vanish. It gets redistributed onto whoever is left, including you. The second sign. New tools arrive with a particular enthusiasm from above.
VISUAL: A bright new device on a pedestal in a meeting room, spotlit and admired.

[A] When leadership is unusually excited about software that does part of your job, listen to how they describe it.
VISUAL: The human seats around that table half empty in the shadow.

[A] The language of doing more with less is the language of a workforce being quietly thinned. The third sign. Your work shifts toward the routine.
VISUAL: A river island shrinking as the water rises around it.

[A] If the interesting, judgment-heavy parts of your role are being centralized or automated, and what remains is the predictable middle.
VISUAL: The high interesting ground of that island washing away first.

[A] You are being slowly positioned as the next role that does not need to exist. And the fourth, subtlest sign. The job stops being talked about as a career.
VISUAL: A career ladder drawn on a wall with the upper rungs faded and erased.

[A] When the language shifts from growth and development to maintenance and coverage, the organization has quietly stopped imagining a future for it.
VISUAL: Only the lower few rungs of that ladder still dark, the top dissolving into plaster.

## PART THREE - HOW TO BE THE ONE WHO STAYS

[A] Now the part that matters. How do you make yourself the person the quiet layoff flows around instead of through.
VISUAL: A stone in a stream that the current parts around, smooth and immovable.

[A] The strategy is not to work harder at the threatened tasks. It is to change what you are made of. First, become the person who uses the tools.
VISUAL: The water flowing past that stone on both sides without touching its core.

[A] Not the person the tools replace. When new artificial intelligence arrives, do not resist it and do not ignore it. Master it faster than anyone around you.
VISUAL: A figure at a console confidently directing a glowing array of automated processes.

[A] Become the colleague who makes the team three times as productive with it. That person does not get thinned. That person gets kept to run it.
VISUAL: That figure in command of the machine rather than competing with it.

[A] Second, move your value toward the parts of the work that resist automation. The judgment calls. The client relationships.
VISUAL: A keystone in an arch glowing slightly brighter than the other stones.

[A] The cross-team coordination that requires trust and context no system holds. Make yourself the human connective tissue whose removal would break something.
VISUAL: The whole arch visibly resting its weight upon that single bright keystone.

[A] Third, make your contribution visible to the people who decide. The quiet layoff is a quiet decision about which roles are essential.
VISUAL: A blueprint of a building with one wall highlighted and labeled as structural.

[A] If your impact is invisible, you are easy to quietly remove. Ensure the decision-makers know what would stop working if you were gone.
VISUAL: Architects gathered around that blueprint, the load-bearing wall clearly marked.

[A] And fourth, keep your options alive on the outside. Build the skills, the network, and the proof of work that make you mobile.
VISUAL: A figure standing at a crossroads with several open roads leading outward.

[A] The cruel paradox is that the most secure employee is the one who could leave tomorrow, because security comes from being wanted in many places.
VISUAL: Each of those roads lit and viable, none of them a dead end.

[A] The quiet layoff is real, and it is spreading, and it will not announce itself. But it flows around the people who made themselves essential and mobile in time.
VISUAL: A single lit office among many dark ones in a tower at night, steady and occupied.

[A] So read the signs where you are, master the tools instead of fearing them, and become the load-bearing wall. The quiet ones get removed quietly. Be impossible to remove.
VISUAL: That one lit window holding its light while the windows around it have gone dark.
