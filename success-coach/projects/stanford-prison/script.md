## PART TWO - HOW THE CONCLUSION CAME FIRST

[A] Look at the design. There was no control condition. No comparison group. A handful of participants. And a researcher who was also the prison superintendent, and deeply invested in the outcome.

[A] The conclusion was announced to the press before any careful analysis. The story was too good to check, and it fitted a moment when everyone wanted to understand atrocity.

[A] And here is the deepest failure. It gave people what they wanted. A theory that explained cruelty while excusing everyone who commits it. The situation did it. Not you. Never you.

## PART THREE - WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENS

[A] So if the situation does not simply overwhelm character, what does the real evidence show? Something more useful, and much harder to hide behind.
VISUAL: A bright clean set of honest findings on a pale surface in daylight, modest and well supported.

[A] People are not passive clay. They are persuaded. The guards who became harsh did so because they were given an identity, a purpose, and an authority who told them it mattered.
VISUAL: A bright person accepting a role they have been offered in clear daylight, deliberate and willing.

[A] That is active, not passive. It is not that they lost themselves. It is that they were recruited into a project, and did their part with commitment.

[CARD: they were not overwhelmed. they were recruited.]

[A] Which is far more frightening than the myth. Because a person who was overwhelmed can be excused. A person who was persuaded chose, and could have chosen otherwise.

[A] The real lesson is not that anyone can become a monster. It is that ordinary people can be talked into cruelty by someone who makes it sound necessary.
