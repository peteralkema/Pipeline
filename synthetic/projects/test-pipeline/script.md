## COLD OPEN

[A] In the spring of two thousand twenty-six, a small team in a quiet office decided to rebuild the way their videos were made.
VISUAL: a dim open-plan office at dawn, empty desks, one monitor glowing, seen from the doorway

[A] They had spent months fighting their own tools — a pipeline that paused the narrator every time a graphic appeared, that cut the voice and resumed it, that fought the very thing it was built to serve. The fix, when it finally came, was not more code. It was less. They stopped treating silence as something to manufacture and let the voice run unbroken, with the pictures conforming to it rather than the other way around.
VISUAL: slow push-in on the single glowing monitor, lines of a script scrolling
