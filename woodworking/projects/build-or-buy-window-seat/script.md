## PART ONE - BUILDING IT YOURSELF

[A] The first route is building it yourself, and it offers the most reward for the most effort. Build your and you get exactly what you want - the perfect fit, the exact design, the features you choose - at the lowest material cost, plus the deep satisfaction of having made it with your hands.
VISUAL: A self-built window seat, made by hand to fit perfectly; clean interior, warm light, no people.

[A] The great gift of building it yourself is fit and control. A homemade seat can be built to your window's exact, irregular shape, with the precise storage and style you want. And since labour is the biggest cost of any built-in, doing it yourself saves the most money of any route by far.
VISUAL: A self-built seat fitting a window's exact shape; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] But it asks real things of you - the skill to build well, the tools, and the patience to see it through. A poorly built seat is worse than none, so this route rewards those with woodworking ability and punishes those without. Be honest about your skills before choosing it.
VISUAL: A self-built seat demanding real skill and tools; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] So building it yourself is the route of best fit, lowest cost, and greatest satisfaction - for those with the skill, tools, and time. For a capable maker, it's almost always the best path. For a beginner tackling a complex built-in, it may be a bridge too far.
VISUAL: A well-built self-made window seat, the maker's reward; clean interior, warm light, no people, no text.

## PART TWO - HAVING IT MADE

[A] The second route is commissioning a carpenter to build it for you. This gives you the custom fit and quality of a built-in without needing the skill yourself - a professional builds exactly the seat you want, fitted perfectly to your window, made to last. The finest result, if you can afford it.
VISUAL: A professionally-made custom built-in window seat; clean interior, warm daylight, no people.

[A] The great gift here's a perfect custom result with none of your labour. A good carpenter delivers the exact fit, the fine finish, and the built-in permanence that make the best window seats, and you need lift nothing but the phone. For those who want the best and lack the skill or time, it's ideal.
VISUAL: A custom seat delivering the best result without your labour; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] But it is the most expensive route by far, because you pay for skilled labour, which is the biggest cost of a built-in. A commissioned window seat can cost several times what building it yourself would, and finding a good carpenter and waiting for the work adds effort and time of its own.
VISUAL: A commissioned seat, the costliest route; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] So having it made is the route of best result and least effort, at the highest cost - for those who want a perfect custom built-in, lack the skill or time, and can afford craft. It buys the finest seat, if your budget can bear the price of skilled hands.
VISUAL: A fine commissioned window seat, worth its cost; clean interior, warm light, no people, no text.

## PART THREE - BUYING READY-MADE

[A] The third route is buying a freestanding window seat ready-made - a bench you purchase and simply place beneath your window. It is the fastest, easiest, and often cheapest route, needing no skill and no waiting, just a purchase and a spot to set it. The path of least effort by far.
VISUAL: A freestanding ready-made window bench placed beneath a window; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] Its gift is speed and simplicity. You choose a bench, buy it, place it, and you are done - no building, no carpenter, no mess or wait. For a renter, a beginner, or anyone who wants a window seat with minimal effort and commitment, buying ready-made is the obvious, painless choice.
VISUAL: A ready-made bench giving a window seat with no effort; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] But you sacrifice fit and that built-in look. A bought bench is a standard size, so it rarely fits your window's exact shape, leaving gaps at the sides and looking like furniture rather than architecture. And your choice is limited to what is sold, not what you might design. Ease costs you fit and character.
VISUAL: A ready-made bench with gaps, clearly furniture; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] So buying ready-made is the route of speed, ease, and low commitment, at the cost of fit and the built-in look - for renters, beginners, and anyone wanting a seat now with no fuss. It gives a seat quickly, if you can accept it will look and fit like furniture.
VISUAL: A ready-made window bench, quick and simple; clean interior, warm light, no people, no text.

## PART FOUR - CHOOSING YOUR ROUTE

[A] So how do you choose? Weigh three things - your skill, your budget, and your need for fit. If you have woodworking ability and want the best fit for the least money, build it yourself. It is the maker's route, and the most rewarding for those able to walk it.
VISUAL: A window seat chosen by the build-it-yourself route; clean interior, warm light, no people.

[A] If you want a perfect custom built-in but lack the skill or time, and can afford it, have it made. If you want a window seat quickly, cheaply, and with no commitment - especially if you rent - buy one ready-made. Match the route to your situation, not to what someone else did.
VISUAL: A window seat matched to its owner's situation; clean interior, soft light, no people.

[A] And know that the routes can blend. Buy a plain freestanding bench and improve its fit with trim to fake a built-in look. Or build the simple parts yourself and have a carpenter tackle the tricky ones. The three paths are not walls but options you can mix to suit your skill and purse.

[A] So weigh your skill, budget, and need for fit, and choose the route that fits your life - build, commission, or buy. The right one is simply the one that suits you. Next time, we'll look at the hidden costs of a window seat. Until then, may you find the route that fits you.
