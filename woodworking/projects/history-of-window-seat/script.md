## PART ONE - BORN OF STONE

[A] The window seat begins with a simple fact of old building - stone walls were enormously thick. A medieval castle wall might be many feet deep, built massive for strength and defence. And when you cut a window through a wall that thick, you don't get a thin opening.

[A] That deep recess was space begging to be used. So builders set stone benches into the sides of it, facing each other across the opening. The window seat was born - not as a comfort, but simply as the natural use of the deep hollow that a window in a thick wall created.

[A] And these early window seats served a serious purpose - light. Before glass was common, windows were small and precious, and the light that fell through them was the best in the whole gloomy, candle-lit hall.
