## PART ONE - WHAT THEY DO

[A] A Forstner bit bores a wide, clean, flat-bottomed hole with smooth sides, guided by a small central point and cutting rim. Unlike an ordinary bit that leaves a pointed bottom and rough sides, the Forstner leaves a hole with a flat floor and clean walls - a precise, tidy recess rather than a rough tunnel.
VISUAL: A Forstner bit boring a clean flat hole; clean workshop, warm light, no people.

[A] Its defining gift is the flat bottom. Many jobs need a hole that stops partway with a flat floor - for a hinge, a recess, an inlay, a peg seat. Ordinary bits leave a pointed cone at the bottom; only the Forstner gives a clean flat floor. For flat-bottomed holes, it is the tool.
VISUAL: A flat-bottomed hole for a hinge; clean workshop, soft light, no people.

[A] It also bores clean, large, and overlapping holes. Forstner bits cut wide holes with smooth edges and little tear-out, can bore at angles, and can overlap to hollow out slots and recesses. This clean, controlled, large-hole boring is well beyond what a twist bit can do neatly. Precision in wide holes is its strength.
VISUAL: A Forstner bit boring a wide clean hole; clean workshop, warm light, no people.

[A] So Forstner bits bore clean, flat-bottomed, smooth-sided holes - wide, precise recesses that ordinary bits cannot make. They turn hole-boring from rough to refined, especially where a flat bottom or a clean wide hole is needed. For proper, tidy holes in fine work, they are the right bits.

## PART THREE - HOW THEY DIFFER FROM ORDINARY BITS

[A] How do Forstner bits differ from the ordinary twist bits most people own? Twist bits are made for speed and deep holes, clearing waste up their spirals, but they leave pointed bottoms, rougher walls, and can wander or tear on wide or shallow holes. They are general drilling bits, not precision boring bits.
VISUAL: A twist bit leaving a pointed rough hole; clean workshop, soft light, no people.

[A] Forstner bits, by contrast, are made for clean, precise, flat-bottomed boring rather than fast deep drilling. They cut a tidy hole with a flat floor and smooth sides, but more slowly and with more effort, and are best used in a drill press for control. They trade speed for cleanliness and precision.
VISUAL: A Forstner bit trading speed for clean holes; clean workshop, warm light, no people.

[A] So the two are complementary, not competing. Twist bits handle everyday drilling - fast holes for screws and clearance; Forstner bits handle precise recessing and clean wide holes. You use twist bits for general work and reach for Forstner bits when a hole must be flat-bottomed, wide, or clean. Each has its own domain.
VISUAL: Twist bits and Forstner bits for different jobs; clean workshop, soft light, no people.

[A] So Forstner bits differ from ordinary bits by trading fast deep drilling for clean, precise, flat-bottomed boring - filling a gap twist bits leave open. They do not replace ordinary bits but complete the set, handling the tidy holes twist bits cannot. Knowing this shows exactly when to reach for each.
