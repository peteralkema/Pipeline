## PART ONE - WHAT IT DOES

[A] A doweling jig guides your drill bit to bore holes at precise, matching positions in two pieces of wood, so wooden pegs called dowels can be glued between them to join the pieces. The jig ensures the holes line up perfectly, which is the whole difficulty of dowel joints. By hand, alignment is nearly impossible.
VISUAL: A doweling jig guiding aligned drill holes; clean workshop, warm light, no people.

[A] The result is a strong, hidden joint. The glued dowels sit inside the joint, invisible from outside, holding the pieces firmly together with the strength of the pegs and the glue around them. It gives a clean, strong join with no visible fasteners - the elegance of hidden joinery at a very low cost.
VISUAL: A strong invisible dowel joint; clean workshop, soft light, no people.

[A] Its key gift is accuracy. Aligning the holes in two pieces so the dowels fit is the hard part of dowel joinery, and the jig solves it, holding the drill exactly where it should go on both pieces. This turns a fiddly, error-prone joint into a reliable, repeatable one anyone can make.
VISUAL: A doweling jig making accurate matched holes; clean workshop, warm light, no people.

[A] So the doweling jig makes accurate, strong, hidden dowel joints using just a drill and pegs, by guiding the holes to align perfectly. It turns difficult dowel joinery into an easy, reliable process. For clean joints with no visible fasteners, made cheaply, it is a simple and effective tool.

## PART THREE - HOW IT COMPARES

[A] How does the doweling jig compare to its pricier cousins? Against the biscuit joiner, dowels give a stronger, more locating joint, though the jig is a little fiddlier to use. Both make hidden joints; the dowel joint is generally stronger, while biscuits are quicker to place. Each has its balance of strength and speed.

[A] Against the domino joiner, the doweling jig is far cheaper but slower and more manual. The domino makes similar strong hidden joints faster and more easily, but costs many times more. For those who cannot justify the domino's price, the doweling jig achieves much the same result with patience instead of money.
