title: The Last Kids of the Old World — If You Were Born Between 1977 and 1985
description: Pull up a chair. If you were born somewhere between 1977 and 1985, you've probably spent your whole life feeling like you don't quite fit — too young for one crowd, too old for the next. There's a reason for that, and it's not in your head. You were the last kids raised in the analog world and the first dropped into the digital one. You remember the before. This one's for you. If you lived it, you already know. And if you didn't... you had to be there.

## THE BEFORE
